# The Clear card and paying at shops

Two ways to pay: **the Clear card**, which works anywhere cards are taken, and **paying a Clear shop
directly** from the app — by scanning its code, by showing yours, or by approving a payment from its
website.

## The Clear card

**The card opens once your identity is verified.** See
[Getting started](./getting-started.md#verifying-your-identity).

### Virtual and physical cards

**Your first card is virtual** and works the moment it exists, online. From the Card page, **New
card** adds another:

- **A virtual card** — online only, issued straight away, with its own number. Nothing is charged for
  it. You can freeze it without touching your physical card.
- **A physical card** — tap, chip and online. It is posted to the home address in **Settings →
  Personal information**, and a card can't be posted until that address is filled in. **It arrives
  frozen.** To activate it, enter the last four digits printed on it.

Every card you hold spends from the same limit, in the same order.

### What the card spends from

You never choose. **The card spends your cash first, then your credit, cheapest tier first**:
Savings-backed, then Asset-backed, then Income-backed, then Clear Boost if you've added it. The Card
page lays this out under **Spends from**, with each tier's rate. How the tiers work is in
[Credit](./credit.md#your-credit-line).

A card purchase that's been approved but hasn't settled counts as used straight away, because you
can't spend it twice.

### Managing a card

- **Freeze** stops new purchases on that card. **Unfreeze** turns it back on, and asks for Face ID
  when payment protection is on. Anything already spent stays where it was.
- **Details** shows the card number when you ask, and hides it again when you close it. This also
  asks for Face ID when payment protection is on.
- **Set a PIN.** The PIN is entered in the card issuer's own field, not Clear's.

The Card page lists the card's own transactions. **Activity** shows everything, card and otherwise.

### If your card is lost, stolen or damaged

**Freeze it first.** Freezing stops new purchases on that card straight away, and it is the one step
the app carries out today.

**Replace this card** is under the card's **Details**. It offers three choices — lost, stolen or
damaged — and says what each would do. **The app doesn't replace a card yet**: choosing one closes
the sheet, and no card is cancelled or posted.

Until it does:

- **Keep the card frozen.** A frozen card stays frozen until you unfreeze it.
- **Get another card from New card.** A new virtual card is issued at once with its own number. A
  new physical card is posted to your home address, arrives frozen, and is activated with the last
  four digits printed on it. Every card spends from the same limit.
- **Dispute anything you didn't make.** See
  [Disputes](./assurance-and-disputes.md#disputes).

Freezing one card doesn't touch your other cards.

### Spending limits and controls

The Card page shows **Controls** — contactless, online payments, ATM withdrawals and international —
and the card's limits per transaction and per day, with **Adjust limits** to change them.

**The app doesn't apply these yet.** In this version the switches and limits change on screen only:
they aren't saved, and the card issuer isn't told, so they don't stop a purchase. The limits shown
are the same for every member, not ones set on your card. What your card can spend is set by your
cash and your credit line, in the order under [What the card spends from](#what-the-card-spends-from).

Clear's server can set a monthly spending limit on a card with the issuer, but the app doesn't use it
yet. To stop a card spending at all, freeze it.

## Paying at a shop

At a Clear shop you don't need the card. The shop asks for an amount and **you approve it on your own
phone** — nothing is taken until you do.

### Scan the shop's code

Tap **Scan** and point your camera at the code on the shop's screen. If the camera isn't available,
type the eight-character code instead. Scanning a Clear Point's code opens that table's bill.

### Show your Clear code

Your Clear code says who you are, not an amount. Hold it up at the counter, the shop enters the
figure, and the charge arrives on your phone. It shows as a banner on Send and Activity, and every
charge a shop has sent you is listed in **Shop charges**.

### Approving a charge

The charge shows the shop, who raised it, when, the amount and what it includes. Then **one
question: pay now, or over time.**

**Pay now** takes the whole amount from Ready to allocate, and there's nothing to clear later. Where
the shop pays Clear a fee on it, the screen says how much. Your card cash (Spendable) is shown but
can't pay a shop yet. If you're
short, the screen tells you how much, and offers to add money or to pay over time instead.

**Pay over time** opens a plan on your term limit. Choose **Next cycle**, or **In 2**, **In 4** or
**In 12** cycles. The sheet shows your first payment and the rate before you approve. Paying over
time is for charges of $50 or more, and some shops offer Pay now only. How plans work is in
[Credit](./credit.md#your-term-limit-and-paying-over-time).

**Decline** is under the pay button. Nothing is charged, and the shop sees it at once.

**A charge you don't answer expires after 24 hours.**

### Paying on a shop's website

A shop that takes Pay with Clear online sends you to an approval sheet in the app. It works the same
way — Pay now or Over time where the shop allows it — and the button says what it will do. Once
you've approved, you're sent back to the shop's site.

## If something went wrong

You can dispute a card payment, or a payment to a Clear shop or partner. See
[Disputes](./assurance-and-disputes.md#disputes).
