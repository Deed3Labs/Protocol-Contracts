# The Clear card

Not available yet.

**The Clear card is Clear's own membership card, made of wood.** It is for paying at Clear shops
without your phone. It is not the [Clear Visa card](./clear-visa-card.md), which you can get today.

## What it is

- **Made by Clear, in wood.** It is your membership card.
- **It carries your Clear Pay QR code**, and a chip you tap, set in a pocket at one edge, with rings
  marking where to tap.
- **Premium editions add a small e-ink screen.**
- **It is meant to gain abilities over time.**

## How paying with it will work

At a Clear shop, you tap the card or the shop scans it. Your phone can stay in your pocket.

- **You set an amount you've approved in advance.** A tap or scan at a Clear shop you've approved,
  within that amount, goes through like a card.
- **Anything else asks you first.** A larger amount, or a shop you haven't approved, sends a
  notification to your phone to approve. That is the same as paying a Clear shop with your phone
  today: see [Approving a charge](./paying-at-shops.md#approving-a-charge).
- **Disputes work as they do for other Clear shop payments.** See
  [Disputes](./assurance-and-disputes.md#disputes).

How the design stands, and what is still to be decided, is in
[Clear card design](../protocol/clear-card-design.md).
