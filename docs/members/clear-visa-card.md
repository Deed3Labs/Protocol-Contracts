# The Clear Visa card

**The Clear Visa card is a Visa card. It spends your cash first, then your credit line, cheapest
tier first.** You never choose which. It works anywhere Visa is taken, online with a virtual card and in
person with a physical one.

It is not the Clear card, Clear's own card, which is planned; see [Your cards](./cards.md).

**The card is not available with real money yet.** It runs in its provider's sandbox only. See
[Status and security](../overview/status-and-security.md).

## Getting a card

**The card opens once your identity is verified.** See
[Getting started](./getting-started.md#verifying-your-identity).

**Your first card is virtual** and works the moment it exists, online. From the Card page, **New
card** adds another:

- **A virtual card** — online only, issued straight away, with its own number. Nothing is charged for
  it. You can freeze it without touching your physical card.
- **A physical card** — tap, chip and online. It is posted to the home address in **Settings →
  Personal information**, and a card can't be posted until that address is filled in. **It arrives
  frozen.** To activate it, enter the last four digits printed on it.

Every card you hold spends from the same limit, in the same order.

## What it spends from

**The card spends your cash first, then your credit, cheapest tier first**: Savings-backed, then
Asset-backed, then Income-backed, then Clear Boost if you've added it. The Card page lays this out
under **Spends from**, with each tier's rate. How the tiers work is in
[Credit](./credit.md#your-credit-line).

A card purchase that's been approved but hasn't settled counts as used straight away, because you
can't spend it twice.

What happens behind a purchase, step by step, is in
[How a Visa card purchase works](../protocol/visa-card-purchases.md).

## Managing a card

- **Freeze** stops new purchases on that card. **Unfreeze** turns it back on, and asks for Face ID
  when payment protection is on. Anything already spent stays where it was.
- **Details** shows the card number when you ask, and hides it again when you close it. This also
  asks for Face ID when payment protection is on.
- **Set a PIN.** The PIN is entered in the card issuer's own field, not Clear's.

The Card page lists the card's own transactions. **Activity** shows everything, card and otherwise.

Not available yet.

## If your card is lost or stolen

**Freeze it first.** Freezing stops new purchases on that card straight away, and the card stays
frozen until you unfreeze it. Freezing one card doesn't touch your other cards.

**Replacing a card.** Not available yet.

To keep spending, add another card from **New card** — see [Getting a card](#getting-a-card).

## Disputes

**Dispute anything you didn't make.** A card payment is decided by the card network's rules, not
Clear's. Clear files it with the network for you, and you pick a reason: charged twice, wrong amount,
didn't get it, and so on. See [Disputes](./assurance-and-disputes.md#disputes).

## Where it works, and where it doesn't yet

| | |
|---|---|
| Online, anywhere Visa is taken | Any card, virtual or physical |
| In person: tap and chip | A physical card |
| Apple Pay and Google Pay | Not available yet |
| With real money | Not available yet |

**At a Clear shop you don't need the card.** You can pay from the app instead, and choose to pay now
or over time. See [Paying at Clear shops](./paying-at-shops.md).
