# Sending money

You can send money to another member, or to someone who hasn't joined Clear yet. The **Send** page
holds your contacts, your Clear code, the way to scan one, and **Request**.

## Who you can send to

**A contact Clear knows an account for is paid directly.** For a member it is a transfer inside the
network: there's no fee, it arrives straight away, and it comes out of Ready to allocate.

**A contact with only a phone number or email gets a claim link.** The money is locked in an escrow,
and they get a text or an email with a link to claim it. While it waits:

- it is held for them alone, and nobody can spend it
- the Send page shows a reminder strip for the oldest send still waiting, with how many days it has
  left before it expires
- after the link expires it can't be claimed, and the escrow will only pay it back to you

Your contacts are in **Settings → Contacts**. A contact without a Clear account shows **Invite**
beside it.

Sending asks for Face ID when payment protection is on. See
[Getting started](./getting-started.md#face-id-for-payments).

## Your Clear code

The code on the Send page, and full screen at **Your code**, says who you are. It doesn't carry an
amount. At a shop counter, the shop enters the amount and you approve it on your phone — see
[Paying at a shop](./card-and-shops.md#paying-at-a-shop).

## Claiming money someone sent you

If someone sent you money with a link:

1. **Open the link.** It shows the amount and that it's ready to claim.
2. **Sign in, or connect a wallet.** You can create a Clear account here.
3. **Enter the six-digit code** sent to the phone number or email the money was sent to. This proves
   the link reached the right person.
4. **Choose how to receive it:**
   - **Debit card** — instant where your card is eligible; if it isn't, you can switch to a bank
     payout
   - **Bank account** — an ACH transfer
   - **Crypto wallet** — a connected wallet, your Clear wallet, or an address you paste
5. The page confirms when it's done, with a reference.

**Joining Clear to claim is free**, and the full amount lands in your account.

A link that has expired can't be claimed. If that happens, ask the sender to send it again.

## Requesting money

**Request**, beside the search box on the Send page, opens a request sheet. Pick the person first,
then the amount, and add a note so they know what it's for. The sheet sets out what a request is:

- they see a request, not a charge
- nothing moves until they approve it
- it expires in 7 days
- you're told when they pay or decline

**The app doesn't send requests yet.** In this version, the **Request** button at the foot of the
sheet doesn't send anything, so the other person isn't told. There is also no screen yet for
answering a request someone sends you.

When requests open, Clear's server is built so that a request can only go to a Clear member it finds
by their phone number or email, never to yourself, and the other person gets a notification saying
who asked and how much.

To ask someone for money today, ask them to send it to you from their own Send page.

## Disputing a send

If something went wrong with money sent between you and another member, you can raise a dispute.
A send that hasn't been claimed yet is held while the dispute is open. See
[Disputes](./assurance-and-disputes.md#disputes).
