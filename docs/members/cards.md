# Your cards

Clear has two cards. **The Clear Visa card** is the one you can get today: a Visa card that works
anywhere Visa is taken. **The Clear card** is Clear's own wooden membership card, for paying at Clear
shops without your phone. It is planned and not available yet.

| | The Clear Visa card | The Clear card (planned) |
|---|---|---|
| Where it works | Anywhere Visa is taken: online with a virtual card, tap and chip with a physical one | Clear shops |
| How a payment is approved | Automatically, when your cash and credit cover the amount | Within an amount you've approved in advance, at a Clear shop you've approved, it goes through like a card. Anything else, you approve on your phone |
| How the shop gets paid | Through the card network, like any Visa card | Not decided yet |
| What it spends from | Your cash first, then your credit line, cheapest tier first | Not decided yet |
| What it's made of | A virtual card number, or a physical card | Wood, with your Clear Pay QR code and a chip you tap |
| Status today | Test network only | Not available yet |
