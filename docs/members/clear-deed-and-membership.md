# Your Clear Deed, membership and settings

## Your Clear Deed

**The Clear Deed is the finish line: the title to your home, the house itself, free of debt.** It
isn't a mortgage, and it isn't bought with a loan. The land under it stays held in common by
members, and your home sits on it under a ground lease.

**Nothing here is an offer of a home.** It describes how the path works.

### 1. Equity credits unlock the ELPA

You earn equity credits by saving and by paying your rent on time. At **15,000 credits** you can
apply for an Equity-Lease Participation Agreement (ELPA): the agreement under which you live in a
Clear home and pay toward owning its structure.

- **Credits are points, and 15,000 is counted on everything you've ever earned.** Signing an ELPA
  doesn't spend them. Once you've signed, you can redeem credits into your Housing Allowance (below),
  and redeeming never lowers the count that got you there.
- **Reaching 15,000 makes you eligible to apply.** It doesn't promise a home in a particular place,
  at a particular price or by a particular date.
- **How homes are offered.** Each home is open for applications for at least 21 days. Members who
  need its accessible features come first, then members moving for a change in household size, a
  disability-related need or damage to their home, then a random draw. When you reached 15,000 and
  how much you've saved don't move you up.
- **No income test or credit check** to sign, and you can cancel within 7 days of signing.

Only credits move you along the path. Your savings balance is your money and stays yours, and
credits can't be withdrawn or cashed in.

**In the app.** Your Savings page shows your **home path**: your credits out of 15,000, a step for
each protection you unlock on the way, and, with auto-save running, the month you're on track for.
Every protection is unlocked by 4,000 credits, and at 15,000 the last step, **Apply for your home
(ELPA)**, is reached and the Savings page reads **Ready · Apply for your home**. Once you've
signed an ELPA, your Housing Allowance appears on the Savings page, with **Redeem credits** and
**Add savings or USDC**.

### 2. Your Housing Allowance

**What redeems your home is your Housing Allowance: your equity balance in it.** It has two parts,
shown as one total.

| Part | What goes in | If you leave before redeeming |
|---|---|---|
| **From credits** | Credits you redeem, at $1 a credit, once you've signed an ELPA. Up to $54,000 over your lifetime. Clear funds it | Never paid in cash. It is kept for 24 months in case you sign for another Clear home, then lapses |
| **Your money** | Savings you choose to apply, CLRUSD or USDC you send from your account, and your monthly equity contribution: you choose it, at least 0.30% of the home's price a month | Comes back to you (see below) |

Your own money goes in only once you've signed an ELPA. Until then it stays in your savings, yours
to withdraw.

**Your Housing Allowance is recorded on the chain,** against your own account, so the Deed contracts
can read it when you redeem. Nobody can transfer it, including you, and the amounts are public, as
savings balances already are. It holds no money itself: what you pay goes toward the home.

Alongside it you pay a **community fee**, which covers ground rent, property taxes, insurance and
shared costs.

- **Your Housing Allowance earns nothing and isn't revalued.** It is exactly what went into it.
- **It stays in the home while you live there.** You can't withdraw it, but it moves with you and
  comes back to you if you leave (see below).

### The price: cost plus 15%

**The price of a Clear home is Clear's documented cost of building it, plus 15%.** It is fixed when
you sign and never goes up, however long you take to reach it. A home offered again keeps its
earlier cost, plus any documented improvements; it is never repriced to the market or to
inflation.

### Redeeming your Clear Deed

**When your Housing Allowance reaches the price, you redeem.** Clear transfers the house to you as
your Clear Deed, and pays the cost of the transfer. The land stays held in common by members: your
ground lease and your community fee continue.

### If things change

- **Missing an equity contribution is never a default.** It only pushes back the day you redeem.
  Only an unpaid community fee can lead to losing the home, and only after notice, a hardship plan
  and a court order. Your Housing Allowance is never kept as a penalty.
- **You can move** to a bigger or smaller Clear home, or another community, at any time before you
  redeem, without reaching 15,000 again. Your whole Housing Allowance moves with you. If it already covers the
  new home's price, you redeem that home when you move in and get the difference back.
- **You can leave** at any time. The part that is your money comes back to you within 90 days, less
  only unpaid community fees and the cost of damage beyond normal wear. It is never less because
  prices fell. The part from credits isn't paid out in cash: it is kept for 24 months in case you
  sign for another Clear home.
- **If you leave membership before signing an ELPA,** your credits end. Your savings are still
  yours.
- **If you die,** a joint member or a member of your household can take over, or the part that is your
  money goes to the beneficiary you name.

### After you redeem

You can sell your home only to Clear or to another member, at a formula price: what you paid, raised
each year by inflation but by no more than 3% a year, or a recent independent appraisal if that is
lower. If no member buys, Clear must.

How credits are earned and how they vest is in
[Savings and equity credits](./savings-and-equity-credits.md#equity-credits).

## Membership plans

**Your savings account is your membership**, as in a credit union. Joining is free, with no buy-in:
what you save is your share in the co-op, and you have one vote however much you save. How
membership and the co-op's rules work is in [Membership and the co-op](../governance/membership.md).

**Everyone is on Standard, and it is free.** On Standard, the multiplier on your rent and bill
credits starts at 1× and rises 0.25× for every six months in a row you pay on time, up to 1.5×.
No plan changes how credits are earned, and your protections don't depend on your plan either. See
[Assurance](./assurance-and-disputes.md#your-protections).

**Accelerated**, an optional paid plan: Not available yet.

## Settings

Open Settings from your avatar. **Sign out**, in the same menu, signs this device out.

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Account** (Personal information) | Your legal name, home address, identity status, phone and email. Your home address is where a physical card is posted. You can change your phone number with a code sent to the new one. Name and date of birth are locked once you're verified |
| **Membership** | The membership agreement, bylaws and patronage, and whether Acceleration is on for you |
| **Security** | Face ID, payment protection and an authenticator app (see [Getting started](./getting-started.md#app-lock-and-face-id)). Trusted devices, login history and recovery contacts are not available yet |
| **Notifications** | What reaches you (below) |
| **Contacts** | People you send to |
| **Linked accounts** | Not available yet (below) |
| **Appearance** | Light, Dusk or Dark |
| **Advanced** | Your wallet address, **Permissions** and **Close account** (below). Downloading your account data or transaction history is not available yet |
| **Help** | Help topics and **Dispute resolution** |

**Your stake in the co-op is your savings balance.** How voting, the bylaws and patronage work is in
[Governance](../governance/README.md).

### Notifications

**Where they arrive.** The bell at the top of every page lists your latest notifications — money
received, credits earned, a payment due and the like — with **Mark all read** and **Clear all**.
Clearing one only takes it off the list: everything that moved money stays in **Activity**.

Once you're signed in, the app asks your device for permission to send notifications. If you allow
it, the most important ones also arrive as push notifications when the app is closed: money
received, credit, milestones, payments due and your identity check.

**The switches in Settings → Notifications.** Not available yet.

To stop push notifications, turn them off for the app in your device's settings.

### Linked accounts

Your account and routing numbers — for direct deposit, or to push money in from your bank — are on
Home, under your cash account's **Account details**. See
[Adding and moving money](./adding-and-moving-money.md#your-account-details).

### Permissions

**Settings → Advanced → Permissions** is where you see what Clear can do without asking each time:

- **Back my credit line with my Savings and Earn automatically.** See
  [Credit](./credit.md#what-backs-your-line-automatically).
- **Auto-clear from deposits**, and what it clears. See [Credit](./credit.md#automatic-repayment).
- **Card spending from Savings**: how much your card may draw against your savings. You can lower it
  or turn it off at any time.
