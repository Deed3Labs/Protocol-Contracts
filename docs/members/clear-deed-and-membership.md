# Your Clear Deed, membership and settings

## Your Clear Deed

**The Clear Deed is the finish line: a home title, free of debt.** It isn't a mortgage, and it isn't
bought with a loan. You earn your way to it with equity credits — by saving, and by paying your rent
on time — and consistency is what gets you there.

**The Clear Deed is reached at 15,000 equity credits.** Your Savings page shows your
**home path**: your credits out of 15,000, a step for each protection you unlock on the way, and,
with auto-save running, the month you're on track for. The last step, at 15,000, is rent assurance
and your Clear Deed together.

When you've reached the end of the path, the Savings page reads **Ready · Your Clear Deed**. The next
step, signing your Equity-Lease Participation Agreement (ELPA), is not available yet.

What counts toward it:

- **Only equity credits.** Your savings balance is your money and stays yours; the credits are what
  move you along the path.
- **Credits can't be withdrawn or cashed in.** They only go toward your Clear Deed.

How credits are earned and how they vest is in
[Savings and equity credits](./savings-and-equity-credits.md#equity-credits).

## Membership plans

There are two membership plans. **The only thing a plan changes is your equity-credit multiplier** —
the number your rent and bill credits are multiplied by.

| | Standard | Accelerated |
|---|---|---|
| Multiplier on rent and bill credits | Starts at 1×, rises 0.25× for every six months in a row you pay on time, up to 1.5× | 1.5× from the start |
| Matched savings credits | 1 per dollar saved | 1 per dollar saved |
| Protections | Unlock on your credits | Unlock on your credits |

**Everyone joins on Standard**, and joining is free. Your protections don't depend on your plan; they
unlock on your equity credits. See [Assurance](./assurance-and-disputes.md#your-protections).

Accelerated is a shortcut, not the only route: Standard reaches the same 1.5× through on-time
payments. **Settings → Membership → Acceleration** shows whether it's on for you. Turning it on is
not available yet.

## Settings

Open Settings from your avatar. **Sign out**, in the same menu, signs this device out.

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Account** (Personal information) | Your legal name, home address, identity status, phone and email. Your home address is where a physical card is posted. You can change your phone number with a code sent to the new one. Name and date of birth are locked once you're verified |
| **Membership** | The membership agreement, bylaws and patronage, and whether Acceleration is on for you |
| **Security** | Face ID, payment protection and an authenticator app (see [Getting started](./getting-started.md#app-lock-and-face-id)). Trusted devices, login history and recovery contacts are not available yet |
| **Notifications** | What reaches you (below) |
| **Contacts** | People you send to |
| **Linked accounts** | Not available yet (below) |
| **Appearance** | Light, Dusk or Dark |
| **Advanced** | Your wallet address, **Permissions** and **Close account** (below). Downloading your account data or transaction history is not available yet |
| **Help** | Help topics and **Dispute resolution** |

**Your stake in the co-op is your savings balance.** How voting, the bylaws and patronage work is in
[Governance](../governance/README.md).

### Notifications

**Where they arrive.** The bell at the top of every page lists your latest notifications — money
received, credits earned, a payment due and the like — with **Mark all read** and **Clear all**.
Clearing one only takes it off the list: everything that moved money stays in **Activity**.

Once you're signed in, the app asks your device for permission to send notifications. If you allow
it, the most important ones also arrive as push notifications when the app is closed: money
received, credit, milestones, payments due and your identity check.

**The switches in Settings → Notifications.** Not available yet.

To stop push notifications, turn them off for the app in your device's settings.

### Linked accounts

Your account and routing numbers — for direct deposit, or to push money in from your bank — are on
Home, under your cash account's **Account details**. See
[Adding and moving money](./adding-and-moving-money.md#your-account-details).

**Settings → Linked accounts.** Not available yet.
