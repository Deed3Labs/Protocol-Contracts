# Your Clear Deed, membership and settings

## Your Clear Deed

**The Clear Deed is the finish line: a home title, free of debt.** It isn't a mortgage, and it isn't
bought with a loan. You earn your way to it with equity credits — by saving, and by paying your rent
on time — and consistency is what gets you there.

**In the app today, the Clear Deed is reached at 15,000 equity credits.** Your Savings page shows your
**home path**: your credits out of 15,000, a step for each protection you unlock on the way, and,
with auto-save running, the month you're on track for. The last step, at 15,000, is rent assurance
and your Clear Deed together.

When you've reached the end of the path, the Savings page reads **Ready · Your Clear Deed**. The step
that follows — **Start the ELPA** — isn't open in the app yet.

What counts toward it:

- **Only equity credits.** Your savings balance is your money and stays yours; the credits are what
  move you along the path.
- **Credits can't be withdrawn or cashed in.** They only go toward your Clear Deed.
- **Credits are forfeited if you close your account**, vested or not.

How credits are earned and how they vest is in
[Savings and equity credits](./savings-and-equity-credits.md#equity-credits).

## Membership plans

There are two membership plans. **The only thing a plan changes is your equity-credit multiplier** —
the number your rent and bill credits are multiplied by.

| | Standard | Accelerated |
|---|---|---|
| Multiplier on rent and bill credits | Starts at 1×, rises 0.25× for every six months in a row you pay on time, up to 1.5× | 1.5× from the start |
| Matched savings credits | 1 per dollar saved | 1 per dollar saved |
| Protections | Unlock on your credits | Unlock on your credits |

**Everyone joins on Standard**, and joining is free. Your protections don't depend on your plan; they
unlock on your equity credits. See [Assurance](./assurance-and-disputes.md#your-protections).

Accelerated is a shortcut, not the only route: Standard reaches the same 1.5× through on-time
payments. **Settings → Membership → Acceleration** shows whether it's on for you. Turning it on isn't
open in the app yet, and nothing is charged.

## Settings

Open Settings from your avatar.

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Account** (Personal information) | Your legal name, home address, identity status, phone and email. Your home address is where a physical card is posted. You can change your phone number with a code sent to the new one. Name and date of birth are locked once you're verified |
| **Membership** | When you joined, and the membership agreement, bylaws, patronage, voting and Acceleration |
| **Security** | Face ID, payment protection and an authenticator app. See [Getting started](./getting-started.md#app-lock-and-face-id) |
| **Contacts** | People you send to, and partners you follow |
| **Advanced** | Your wallet address, downloads, and **Permissions** |
| **Help** | Help topics and **Dispute resolution** |

**Your stake in the co-op is your savings balance.** How voting, the bylaws and patronage work is in
[Governance](../governance/README.md).

### Permissions

**Settings → Advanced → Permissions** lists what Clear can do without asking you each time, such as
saving automatically or moving savings back to cash. Turning one off closes nothing; Clear just asks
next time.

It also has the **Repay from USDC deposits** switch for automatic repayment. See
[Credit](./credit.md#automatic-repayment).

### Closing your account

**Close account** is under Advanced. Before anything happens, it sets out what closing does:

- credit you owe must be cleared before you can close
- your savings and cash are paid out to you
- your equity credits are forfeited, vested or not

Nothing happens until you confirm on the next screen.
