How patronage works

Patronage is your share of the co-op's surplus, based on how much you used it — not how much you saved.

What counts as activity

1. At year end the co-op totals its surplus after costs and reserves.

2. Members vote on how much is distributed versus reinvested.

3. The distributed portion is split in proportion to each member's patronage basis.

4. Your share lands in your cash account.

Saving more doesn't increase your patronage — it increases your credit limit and your progress toward a home. Different mechanisms.

Dispute resolution

Disputes between members are handled by an independent third party, administered by The Deed & Title Co.

1. Raise it directly with the other member first, in the app.

2. If that doesn't resolve it, open a case. Both sides submit their account.

3. A neutral third party reviews and decides. The co-op administers but doesn't judge.

4. The decision is recorded and binding under the membership agreement.
