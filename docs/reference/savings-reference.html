States

  • Day one: $0.00, ladder shown with Cycle assurance on, "Set up auto-save" in the path footer, an empty bar in what you hold.
  • Saving: one next step in cobalt, filled to its progress; vesting in amber.
  • Paused: savings backing spending stops the vesting clock. The date, the next step and the vesting row turn amber; the next-unlock bar offers Top off.
  • Path complete: every step and protection on; the bar hands off to the Clear Deed.
  • Nothing vesting: drop the amber segment and row, never show $0.
  • Phone has no buttons under the balance. Move money is the floating button; Auto-save sits behind Change.
