Merchant onboarding.

A shop signs itself up, on the back-office computer or the counter tablet, in about ten minutes, and can take its first Clear charge the same day. A rep may sit beside the first few shops, but nothing here needs one.

What it is for

  • Only what a Clear charge needs. Who you are, your shop, your team, your terms, verifying the business, where payouts go, and training the counter. Cards, readers, items, the drawer, tips and discounts are not in signup; they are a checklist on Home afterwards, because every one of them is optional and each is already a screen in Settings or Inventory.
  • The address does the tax. A shop never types a rate. The address sets it, and what the shop sells and its typical sale set sensible defaults, all changeable later.
  • A code shows what it changes before it is applied. Standard terms are the default; a founding code lowers both Clear rates, and says honestly how many places are left.
  • Verification does not block the flow. The shop keeps going while Bridge checks; only payouts wait for it.
  • Training is a step, not an email. Signing takes four minutes and any owner will do it. Training the counter takes fifteen and is what decides whether the shop actually uses Clear.

6 · Where payouts go

One business account, linked with Plaid through the bank’s own sign-in, in the name the business verified under; Plaid confirms the names match. Open

  • Verifying twice. Bridge verifies the business at signup, and Stripe verifies it again when the shop connects cards. The two cannot share one check, so the Stripe step should be pre-filled with what the shop already entered.
  • Business accounts and cards. If shops get the same accounts and cards as members, cards would come through Lithic, as they do for members. Not in signup until then.
  • Practice mode. The $1 practice charge needs a practice path through the charge flow that never touches the ledger or payouts.
  • Codes beyond founding. A regional launch or a partner-referred code would reuse the same terms table with different right-hand values.
