Settings.

The merchant app’s Settings, on the member app’s anatomy: a rail and a pane on the landscape tablet, an index and pushed pages everywhere narrower, and modals only for actions.

The decisions

  • Sub-pages are panes. Only actions are modals. The member app’s rule, carried over. Shop hours, the listing, notifications and terms are places you go, so they are panes. Changing the payout account, adding a device and leaving are things you do, so they are modals.
  • The rail is drawn with rules, not chips. Selected is ink, semibold, with a 2px left rule, exactly as in the member app. Help sits under a hairline, as it does there.
  • Seven sections, named for what an owner is looking for. Shop, Payouts, Partnership, Counter, Security, Notifications, Advanced, then Help. Partnership is the merchant’s Membership: terms, the agreement and the co-op record.
  • Every pane is cells. Header, main, footer, same as anything in a slab. A pane’s one action sits in the footer beside the sentence that explains it, never in a tinted box.
  • Staff things stay on Staff. Roles, the refund limit and people’s hours are about people, so there is no Staff section here.

Settings · owner · tablet

The shop leads, as the person does in the member app: its initials, its name, and one line saying who it is and since when. Then the rail and the pane. Shop opens first, because what members see is what a shop was sold. Hours read as plain rows here and change on their own pane, because Staff and the listing both read from them.

Open

  • What partner membership gives a shop. The co-op record shows the date and the code used. What membership lets a shop do, such as a vote or a share, is not designed yet.
  • Whether a returning shop keeps its founding rate. The confirm says it does not. That is the honest default for scarce terms, but it is your call, and it is the line most likely to make an owner stay.
  • What a manager sees. Probably the counter view plus Payouts read-only, since a manager can send payouts but not change where they go.
  • Closing early today. Members see “open now” from shop hours. A shop that closes early without changing them sends members to a locked door, so a same-day switch on Home may matter more than dated closures.
