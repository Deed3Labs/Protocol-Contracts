The shop app: a shop’s rewards, on a Clear account.

An app a shop can hand its customers, in the shop’s own name and colours: their points, the shop’s rewards and offers, Scan to pay, and their Clear balance. It is not a new product underneath. It is the member app cut down to one shop, on the same account, the same API and the same money, so anything a customer does in it shows up in the Clear app and in every other shop’s app. Drawn for Mi Casa Tacos and Mike’s Tire with one customer, Sofia, who uses both.

The decisions

  • One codebase, a config per shop. Not a fork per shop. A fork per merchant means every fix, every security patch and every change to how money moves has to be merged into hundreds of repos, and the ones that don’t merge drift away from the API. The shop’s name, logo, colours, fonts, earning rules, rewards and offers are data; one app reads them. It lives in the monorepo as apps/rewards, beside apps/member and apps/merchant, sharing packages/ (contracts, domain, tokens) and the API. No template repo for now.
  • One person, one Clear sign-in, everywhere, and a member never signs up twice. Joining Mi Casa’s rewards with a phone number creates the same identity the Clear app uses. Signing in to Mike’s Tire’s app, or the Clear app, finds the same person, the same balance and every shop’s points. Nothing is ever copied between apps, so nothing can disagree.
  • Two levels, and the second is never a wall. With just a phone number a customer earns and redeems points, sees offers and pays by card at the shop. Paying from a Clear balance or over time needs Clear onboarding. Where that is the next step, the app says what it unlocks and opens the same onboarding the Clear app uses, then returns to the screen they were on. The API’s existing capability checks decide which, so the app never guesses.
  • The shop’s brand around, Clear’s inside. Everything about the shop wears the shop’s colours. The balance block always wears Clear’s paper and mark, and says it is a Clear account. The shop never holds a customer’s money and the app must never look like it does.
  • This shop only, in this shop’s app. Activity shows purchases here; the balance is the whole balance, because it is theirs. Purchases at other shops are one tap away in Clear, never listed in a competitor’s app. The API enforces it, not the screen.
  • Points are the shop’s and are never money. They buy items and discounts at that shop only, can’t be cashed out, sent or moved between shops. That keeps rewards out of gift-card and stored-value rules (to confirm with counsel) and keeps the shop in charge of its own promotions.
  • A reward is the order’s one discount. Orders already carry one discount, a code or a manual one. A reward becomes the third kind. Taxes, refunds and reports already know what to do with it, and a reward can’t be stacked on a code.
  • Two front doors, one app. A shop’s own link, useclear.org/s/micasa, is only ever that shop: its brand, its own home-screen icon. rewards.useclear.org is every shop a person has joined, in Clear’s look, with a switcher; open a shop there and it takes on that shop’s look. Same code, same screens; the address decides the scope. Both are web apps: installable, no store review, live the day the shop turns it on.
  • Two paid add-ons, both revenue. The shop’s own domain (rewards.micasatacos.com), and the shop’s own app in the App Store and Google Play, which Clear builds, submits and keeps updated. Both stores require a template-built app to be published from the shop’s own developer account, not ours (Apple 4.2.6; Google Play’s spam policy), so the service runs on the shop’s accounts with Clear invited in to do the work.

1 · How it fits

Three apps, one account system. The Clear app is the whole account. Rewards is a window onto it, either one shop’s or all of them. The merchant app is the shop’s counter. They share the API, the identity, the balance and the order records, so a payment made in Mi Casa’s app is a normal order at Mi Casa’s counter and a normal payment in Sofia’s Clear activity.

Signing up, and You

Sign-up is a phone number, a code and a first name. It is not Clear onboarding, simplified or otherwise: rewards need no identity check. Clear onboarding stays separate and optional, offered only where a balance would help. A Clear member signs in, never signs up. The same sign-in methods as the Clear app sit under the phone field, so someone who joined Clear with email, Google or Apple uses that and lands in their account. And a phone number that isn’t on any account gets one question once its code is confirmed and before anything is created, Already have Clear? It comes after the code so the question never tells a stranger whether a number has an account; it is asked because a member who never added a phone would otherwise end up with a second, empty account. Yes signs them in their usual way and adds the number to that account, so the phone works next time. No goes on to a first name.

Jen at the counter, Sofia at Table 6

10 · Getting it onto phones

Three ways onto a phone, two of them paid, and where the code lives. Most shops only ever need the first.

Web apps · included

A shop’s link, useclear.org/s/micasa, and the hub, rewards.useclear.org, both served by one Vercel project for apps/rewards. The apex site (served by apps/member, which already handles /p/ and /r/) forwards /s/* to it with one rewrite, so no wildcard DNS is needed. A sign-in cookie on .useclear.org covers useclear.org and rewards.useclear.org, so a person signed in at the hub is signed in at every shop’s link. Each shop’s link has its own manifest and scope, so it installs as its own icon. On iPhone, web push works for installed web apps (iOS 16.4 and later). Every shop gets every fix the same day.

Own domain · paid add-on

rewards.micasatacos.com, or any domain the shop owns. Added to the Vercel project through its domains API, with HTTPS issued automatically once the shop’s DNS record is in. The host picks the shop, as the path does. The cookie can’t cross to another domain, so customers confirm their number once there. Cheap for us to run; the price is for the convenience and the brand.

Store app · paid add-on

The same codebase wrapped with Capacitor, as the merchant app is, built per shop by a pipeline from the shop’s config. Apple (guideline 4.2.6) and Google Play (its spam policy) both reject template-built apps submitted by the service on a client’s behalf, so each shop opens its own developer accounts (Apple yearly, Google one-time, both as a business) and invites Clear to build, submit and update. Apple also rejects apps that are only a repackaged website (4.2), so the store build must carry native value: push, the rewards card in Apple and Google Wallet, and the native scanner. Price it as a service: setup, then monthly, because review rejections and new phone versions are ongoing work.

In the monorepo · apps/rewards

A Vite app like the member app, importing the same contracts, domain and token packages, so a change to an order or a balance type breaks it at build time instead of in a shop’s customer’s hand. The host and path pick the scope and the shop. It joins the build rules from the Vercel work: it builds only when apps/rewards or a package it imports changes. The Capacitor builds run in CI from the same code, one config per shop.

11 · Behind the screens

Most of it exists. Identity, capabilities, balances, the phone pay page, orders and discount codes are already in the API. The new parts are the shop app’s config, a points ledger, the rewards list, and a token that sees one shop.

Front doors

useclear.org/s/:slug and a shop’s own domain are one shop; rewards.useclear.org is the member’s shops. Same build, same routes; the shop-scoped session (below) is issued per shop either way, so the hub holds one per shop the member has joined. Own domains are a table (domain, shop, status, verified at) checked on every request.

Resolution in apps/rewards; GET /api/s/:slug, GET /api/rewards/me/shops, POST /api/merchant/shop-app/domain

Store builds

One build record per shop and platform: the bundle id, version, the config it was built from, review status. A CI job builds from the shop’s config; push and Wallet passes need per-shop keys stored with it.

GET /api/merchant/shop-app/store; CI workflow, not the API, does the building

Shop app config

One row per shop: slug, on or off, logo, colour, heading and text fonts, paper, the points name, which offers show. Uploaded fonts go to the shop’s asset store after the checks (glyphs, legibility, size) and are converted to WOFF2; the licence is the shop’s declaration, stored with the upload. Public and cached; it is everything the app needs to dress itself.

GET /api/s/:slug (public), GET/PATCH /api/merchant/shop-app

Joining

The same provider and the same sign-in methods as the Clear app (phone, email, Google, Apple), so a Clear member always lands in their own account. A phone number on no account is asked Already have Clear? first; yes links the number to their existing account instead of creating one. Joining a shop records consent to share name, number and purchases with that shop, and nothing else. Leaving deletes the points and the consent.

POST /api/s/:slug/join (also after a payment, crediting it), DELETE /api/s/:slug/membership, GET /api/partners?near= for Add a shop

A token that sees one shop

The app’s session is scoped to (member, shop). It can read the member’s balance and capabilities, this shop’s orders, points, rewards and offers, and call the pay endpoints. It can’t list other shops’ activity. The same rule binds a forked template.

Scope on the session; enforced in middleware next to requireMemberCapability

Points ledger

Append-only entries per (shop, member): earned on a paid order, used by a reward, reversed by a refund, a bonus (joining, birthday, a friend), adjusted by staff with a reason, or expired. The balance is the sum, never a stored number. Earn rules are versioned config: by dollars or visits, the rate, keep-the-change (a per-member remainder), boosts with days, hours or categories (the biggest applies), the paying-with-Clear multiplier, bonuses and expiry. Each entry stores the rule version that produced it, so a change never rewrites history.

GET /api/s/:slug/me, loyalty_entries

Rewards and redeeming

Rewards are an item or an amount off, at a points price. Using one adds an OrderDiscount of a new kind, reward, with the reward’s id; the one-discount rule stands. A counter code is a short-lived claim that becomes that discount when scanned; points come off when the order is paid.

POST /api/s/:slug/rewards/:id/claim, DiscountRequest gains { kind: 'reward' }

Offers

Existing discount codes gain showInApp and an optional audience (everyone, members, Clear members). Applying one from the app is the existing code path.

Settings › Discounts, unchanged otherwise

Paying

The existing phone pay page: a Clear tender for members who can, a card tender on the shop’s Stripe for everyone else, now accepting a reward id.

POST /api/p/:code/pay

Open

  • Price. The web apps and points are included: every shop’s app is a door into Clear for its customers, and charging for that would close it. The two add-ons are where it earns: own domain as a small monthly fee, the store app as a setup fee plus monthly. Square sells loyalty as a separate subscription per location, so shops are used to paying for this.
  • Counsel, before launch: that points as drawn (shop-funded, item or discount only, no cash value, never transferable) stay outside gift-card and stored-value rules in the first states; and the wording of the consent line.
  • Cross-shop perks (something Clear gives for spending at any Clear shop) are possible on this ledger but are Clear’s promotion, not a shop’s, and should wait.
  • Shops already on Square or Toast loyalty need an import of points by phone number, or they won’t switch.
