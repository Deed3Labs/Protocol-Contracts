# How contributing works

Contributing land or property to Clear, step by step. Nothing here is an offer or tax or legal
advice; see [Contributors](./README.md).

## Who holds contributed property

**A contribution is made to a company, not to the co-op's members.** Contributed property is held by
**Clear Properties**, a company under **Clear Capital Holdings**, and the contributor receives shares
in Clear Properties.

The companies, their boards, and the five reserved matters that need the co-op's agreement are set
out in [The co-op's structure](../governance/structure.md). That structure is a draft until the co-op
adopts its bylaws.

## The four steps

A contribution is four steps, and none of them is a sale.

1. **The property goes in.** The deed goes into a nominee trust, with Clear Properties as its
   beneficiary, instead of to a buyer.
2. **The contributor receives shares.** One share for each dollar of the property's appraised value.
   Nothing is settled in cash. See [What shares are](./shares.md).
3. **The property's income is handled at the property.** Rent is earned at the property, which pays
   its own costs first; what remains is distributed to every holder pro rata. See
   [What contributors receive](./what-contributors-receive.md).
4. **Everyone holds on the same terms.** Clear Capital's own position is in the same pool, with no
   priority and no preferred return.

The steps are the same at any size, from one property to a portfolio. What changes with size is the
diligence, not the structure.

## What kinds of property

Rental property, ideally paid off, and raw land. A backyard is handled differently, as a ground lease
rather than a contribution.

## A contribution is meant to be held

**A contribution is designed to be held, not traded.** If you plan to move property in and out every
couple of years, this structure is not for you. See [Tax basics](./tax-basics.md#the-holding-period).

## How it starts

**With a private conversation.** Clear publishes no terms, rate or projected return. Anything further is
discussed privately, with documents sent to the contributor's own advisers.

## On the chain

**No step of a contribution happens on the chain.**

- The nominee trust, the appraisal and the issue of shares all happen off the chain.
- Recording a contributed property as a T-Deed, the protocol's on-chain title record: Not available
  yet. What a T-Deed is: [Deeds and real-world assets](../overview/deeds-and-real-world-assets.md).
- The Deed track's subdividing and fractionalizing are not used for contributions. See
  [What shares are](./shares.md#not-the-same-as-fraction-tokens).
