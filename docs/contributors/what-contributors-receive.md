# What contributors receive

What the public website says a contributor receives, and what they do not. It sets out mechanics
only: the website publishes no rate, terms or projected return, and neither do these pages. Not an
offer, and not tax or legal advice; see the notice in the [section index](./README.md).
[COUNSEL TO REVIEW]

## Shares

**What a contributor receives for the property is shares in Clear Properties**, one per dollar of
appraised value, and nothing in cash. See [What shares are](./shares.md).

## Distributions, as described

The website describes the order in which a property's income is handled:

1. Rent is earned at the property.
2. The property pays its own costs first: taxes, insurance, upkeep and management.
3. The fund sets reserves and takes its fee.
4. The rest is distributed to every holder, pro rata to shares held.

The website gives no figure for any of these, and says it does not publish terms, rates or projected
returns. How much, if anything, is distributed is not described.

## Tax distributions

**The website says the agreement requires tax distributions**, so that no holder is handed a K-1 for
income they did not receive in cash. The agreement itself is not public and not in this repository.
[COUNSEL TO REVIEW]

## Paperwork

The website says a contributor receives a K-1 rather than a 1099, that it arrives later, and that an
accountant will charge to handle it. See [Tax basics](./tax-basics.md#the-k-1). [COUNSEL TO REVIEW]

## What a contributor does not receive

As the website describes it:

- **No cash at contribution.** Nothing is settled in cash. [COUNSEL TO REVIEW]
- **No control of the properties.** The contributor does not manage or decide about them; the website
  says tenants and repairs are no longer the contributor's.
- **No priority and no preferred return.** Every holder is on the same terms, Clear Capital
  included.
- **No published terms.** The website says anything further is in a private conversation, with
  documents sent to the contributor's advisers.

Who decides about the properties — including the five reserved matters the website says need the
co-op — is in [Governance → The co-op's structure](../governance/structure.md#the-five-reserved-matters),
a draft until the bylaws are adopted.

## What anyone can check on the chain

The website says balances, credit lines and title records run on public, open-source contracts. In
this repository:

- **Title records:** T-Deeds from `DeedNFT`, deployed on Base Sepolia, a test network. No service
  mints them yet. See [Deeds and real-world assets](../overview/deeds-and-real-world-assets.md).
- **Balances and credit lines:** the savings and credit contracts. What anyone can read is in
  [What anyone can read](../investors/on-chain-data.md).
- **Distributions to property holders:** none. No contract pays them, so there is nothing on the
  chain to check.

The website also says the contracts are held by an ownerless foundation. Who holds the keys to each
contract today is recorded in [On-chain authority](../governance/on-chain-authority.md), which does
not describe a foundation.
