# What contributors receive

What a contributor receives, and what they do not. Clear publishes no rate, terms or projected
return. Nothing here is an offer or tax or legal advice; see [Contributors](./README.md).

## Shares

**What a contributor receives for the property is shares in Clear Properties**, one per dollar of
appraised value, and nothing in cash. See [What shares are](./shares.md).

## Distributions

A property's income is handled in this order:

1. Rent is earned at the property.
2. The property pays its own costs first: taxes, insurance, upkeep and management.
3. The fund sets reserves and takes its fee.
4. The rest is distributed to every holder, pro rata to shares held.

No figure is published for any of these steps. How much, if anything, is distributed is not
published either.

## Tax distributions

**The agreement that governs the shares requires tax distributions.** The agreement is not public,
and how a holder is taxed is a question for their own adviser. See [Tax basics](./tax-basics.md).

## What a contributor does not receive

- **No cash at contribution.** Nothing is settled in cash.
- **No control of the properties.** Tenants, repairs and decisions about the properties are no
  longer the contributor's.
- **No priority and no preferred return.** Every holder is on the same terms, Clear Capital
  included.
- **No published terms.** Anything further happens in a private conversation, with documents sent to
  the contributor's own advisers.

Who decides about the properties, including the five reserved matters that need the co-op, is in
[The co-op's structure](../governance/structure.md). That structure is a draft until the co-op
adopts its bylaws.

- **Title records for contributed property:** Not available yet. What a T-Deed is:
  [Deeds and real-world assets](../overview/deeds-and-real-world-assets.md).
- **Distributions to property holders:** none are paid on the chain, so there is nothing there to
