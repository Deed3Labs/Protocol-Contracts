This BurnerBond Collateral Agency Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is made effective [●] (the "**Effective Date**") between **Clear Lending Co.**, a Wyoming limited liability company ("**Clear Lending**"), and **[●]**, a [●] (the "**Collateral Agent**" or "**Agent**"), as collateral agent for the ratable benefit of the Holders of all Series of BurnerBonds. This Agreement is the collateral agency and control agreement that Section 8.1 of IV-01 calls the "Collateral Agency Agreement".

## Recitals

A. Clear Lending issues BurnerBonds to eligible Members under the BurnerBond Terms and Note Purchase Agreement (IV-01). The proceeds of every BurnerBond are paid into the BondVault, which must hold the Redemption Reserve.

B. Section 4.4 of L-01 and Article 8 of IV-01 require Clear Lending to grant a first-priority security interest in the BondVault, the Redemption Reserve and their proceeds, held by an independent collateral agent for the Holders of all Series ratably.

C. The Agent is a bank or trust company that is not an Affiliate of Clear. Each Holder, by buying a BurnerBond, appointed the Agent under Section 8.1 of IV-01.

D. The Lending Board approved this Agreement on [●], with the vote of its Independent Manager.

**Now, therefore,** the parties agree as follows.

## Article 2. Appointment

**2.1 Appointment.** Clear Lending confirms, and the Agent accepts, the Agent's appointment by the Holders under Section 8.1 of IV-01 as collateral agent for the Holders of all Series, to hold the security interest granted in Article 3 and to exercise the rights and perform the duties stated in this Agreement and IV-01.

**2.2 For All Holders Ratably.** The Agent acts for the Holders of all Series equally and ratably, with no priority among Series or Holders, as Section 8.5 of IV-01 provides. The Agent does not act for Clear Lending or any of its Affiliates.

**2.3 Holders' Rights.** Each Holder is an intended third-party beneficiary of this Agreement. Holders act under it only through the Agent and by Direction, except as Sections 11.7 and 11.8 of IV-01 allow.

**2.4 Eligibility.** The Agent must at all times be a bank or trust company organized under the laws of the United States or a State, subject to supervision or examination by a federal or state banking authority, authorized to exercise trust or custody powers, able to hold and use digital asset keys under its own controls, with capital and surplus of at least ten million dollars ($10,000,000) or the minimum its charter requires, whichever is greater, and not an Affiliate of Clear, Clear Capital Holdings or Clear Lending. An Agent that ceases to be eligible will resign under Article 11.

**2.5 Trust Indenture Act.** The Bonds are sold in transactions exempt under Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act of 1933 and Rule 506(c) or Rule 506(b), as IV-03 describes. Under Section 304(b) of the Trust Indenture Act of 1939, no indenture is qualified. This Agreement is not an indenture qualified under that Act, and the Agent is not an indenture trustee. The rights of Holders to payment in Section 11.8 of IV-01 apply as stated there.

## Article 3. Security Interest

**3.1 Grant.** To secure the Secured Obligations, Clear Lending grants to the Agent, for the ratable benefit of the Holders of all Series, a continuing first-priority security interest in all of Clear Lending's right, title and interest, now owned or later acquired, in:

(a) the BondVault and all USDC and other assets held in or credited to it from time to time;

(b) the Redemption Reserve;

(c) the Collateral Deposit Account and all funds in it;

(d) Clear Lending's rights under the BondVault's code to the assets in clauses (a) to (c); and

(e) all proceeds of the foregoing,

(together, the "**Collateral**"). This is the grant in Section 8.2 of IV-01 and Section 4.4 of L-01. It does not cover the BurnerBond token contract, any Bond, Clear Lending's loans or any other asset.

**3.2 Release of Deployable Excess.** USDC withdrawn from the BondVault as deployable excess under Section 7.3 of IV-01, while no Enforcement Notice is in effect and in compliance with Exhibit A, is released from the security interest when it leaves the BondVault. No other release occurs except under Section 3.7 or an amendment made under Article 12.

**3.3 Payments to Holders.** USDC paid from the BondVault to a Holder or Successor is released when paid. A roll releases nothing, because the new Purchase Price stays in the BondVault.

**3.4 Perfection by Filing.** Clear Lending authorizes the Agent to file, and has filed, a financing statement with the Wyoming Secretary of State (filing number [●]) describing the Collateral, and will file continuation statements and amendments as needed. Clear Lending is a Wyoming registered organization and will not change its name, form or jurisdiction without thirty (30) days' prior notice to the Agent.

**3.5 Perfection by Control.**

(a) *Control security agreement.* This Agreement is a control security agreement under W.S. 34-29-103(b) and a security agreement under Article 9 of the UCC.

(b) *Digital assets.* The Agent has control of the USDC in the BondVault under W.S. 34-29-103 through the Agent Role, by which the Agent may at any time prevent every transfer out of the BondVault other than a payment to Holders, and after an Enforcement Notice has the exclusive authority to direct every transfer out of it. To the extent Article 12 of the Uniform Commercial Code, as adopted in any jurisdiction whose law governs, applies to the Collateral, the parties intend that the Agent has control under Section 12-105 through the same arrangement, and Clear Lending acknowledges that the Agent's power is exclusive within the meaning of that Section even though Clear Lending may withdraw deployable excess before an Enforcement Notice.

(c) *Dollars.* Any Collateral held as dollars, including dollars received on redemption or sale of USDC during an Enforcement Period, will be held in a deposit account maintained with the Agent in the Agent's name as collateral agent for the Holders (the "**Collateral Deposit Account**"), giving the Agent control under W.S. 34.1-9-104(a)(3). If the Agent is not a depositary bank, the account will be maintained at a bank under a deposit account control agreement in favor of the Agent under which the bank follows only the Agent's instructions. Clear Lending holds no Collateral in dollars before an Enforcement Notice.

**3.6 Further Assurances.** Clear Lending will take every action the Agent reasonably requests to create, perfect, keep first in priority and enforce the security interest, including signing further control agreements and making code changes the Agent requests under Section 4.5 to preserve control.

**3.7 Termination and Release.** The security interest ends, and the Agent will release the Agent Role and file a termination statement within ten (10) Business Days, when every Bond has been paid in full or cancelled, no Bond is outstanding, Clear Lending has certified that it will issue no further Bonds secured by this Agreement, and every amount due to the Agent has been paid.

## Article 4. The BondVault and the Agent Role

**4.1 Exhibit A.** The BondVault gives the Agent Key the Agent Role described in Exhibit A, and no wider power. Exhibit A is part of this Agreement. Clear Lending represents that the BondVault as deployed implements Exhibit A and passed the independent audit IV-03 requires.

**4.2 No Other Lien or Control.** Clear Lending will not grant any other person a lien on or control over the Collateral, or any role in the BondVault other than those in Exhibit A. This adds to Sections 8.6 and 10.2 of IV-01 (including the negative pledge), which the Agent may enforce on a Direction.

**4.3 Agent Key Custody.** The Agent will hold the Agent Key only in its own custody systems, in a hardware security module or multi-party computation arrangement, under written controls requiring approval by at least two (2) of its authorized officers for every transaction signed with it. The Agent will not share the Agent Key with Clear Lending or any Affiliate of Clear, and Clear Lending will never hold, generate or back up it.

**4.4 Monitoring.** The Agent will run automated monitoring of the BondVault and is treated as knowing what it shows, including any matured Bond left unpaid and any proposed change in its timelock queue. Otherwise the Agent is treated as knowing of an Event of Default only when it receives notice of it.

**4.5 Changes to the BondVault.** No upgrade, replacement, role change or parameter change of the BondVault may take effect without (a) a timelock of at least seven (7) days and two-person control by Clear Lending under Section 10.6 of IV-01, and (b) the Agent's co-signature through the Agent Key. The Agent will co-sign within five (5) Business Days after receiving the proposal, an officer's certificate of Clear Lending, and, for any code change, an independent auditor's report on the change, if the change is:

(a) required by law, or one Section 12.3 of IV-01 allows without Holder consent, and the certificate states that it does not adversely affect any Holder;

(b) approved by the Holder consent Article 12 of IV-01 requires, as certified with the voting record; or

(c) requested by the Agent to preserve its control or the Collateral.

The Agent will not co-sign any change that would lower the floors in Section 7.2 of IV-01, widen the destinations to which assets can leave the BondVault, give anyone power to move a Bond or a Member's assets, or reduce the Agent Role, without the consent of each affected Holder. The Agent may rely on the certificate and report without independent review of code.

**4.6 Rotation of the Agent Key.** The Agent may replace the Agent Key with another address in its custody by a transaction signed with the current Agent Key, effective after forty-eight (48) hours, on notice to Clear Lending. If the Agent Key is lost or compromised, the Agent will notify Clear Lending and Holders under Section 8.1 at once, and the replacement procedure in Exhibit A, Part 4 applies.

## Article 5. Clear Lending's Covenants and Reports

**5.1 Covenants.** Until the security interest ends, Clear Lending will:

(a) keep the Redemption Reserve at or above Section 7.2 of IV-01 and cure any shortfall within two (2) Business Days;

(b) pay every Bond's Maturity Amount when due, from the BondVault or as Section 4.5 of IV-01 provides;

(c) keep the BondVault holding only USDC, and keep it free of any path to the AssurancePool, the Loss Reserve or any other contract, as Section 7.5 of IV-01 requires;

(d) maintain in the App a function by which Holders may sign a Direction by Wallet Signature, deliver every signed Direction to the Agent unaltered within one (1) Business Day, and never block, delay or edit one; and

(e) deliver to Holders, in the App and by email, every notice the Agent asks it to deliver, unaltered, within one (1) Business Day.

**5.2 Reports.** Clear Lending will deliver to the Agent:

(a) within ten (10) Business Days after each month, a collateral report showing, for each Series, Bonds outstanding, Maturity Amounts and Accreted Value, the required and actual Redemption Reserve, deployable excess withdrawn, any Reserve Shortfall and its cure, and Bonds maturing in the next ninety (90) days, showing which have a Roll Confirmation in effect;

(b) within forty-five (45) days after each fiscal quarter, an officer's certificate confirming compliance with Articles 7, 8 and 10 of IV-01 and this Agreement, with the current list of Excluded Bonds;

(c) within one hundred twenty (120) days after each fiscal year, Clear Lending's audited financial statements;

(d) within one (1) Business Day, notice of any Event of Default, Reserve Shortfall, proposed change under Section 4.5, loss, suspension or revocation of its California Financing Law licence, regulatory or court action affecting the Collateral, or security incident affecting the BondVault; and

(e) within two (2) Business Days after request, a list of Holders and their Bonds as of any record time, with their contact details and any registered Successor, for use only under this Agreement.

## Article 6. Directions

**6.1 Who May Direct.** The Required Holders may direct the time, method and place of any proceeding or remedy available to the Agent, or of the exercise of any power conferred on it, by a Direction substantially in the form of Exhibit C.

**6.2 How Directions Are Given.** Holders sign a Direction by Wallet Signature through the function in Section 5.1(d), or by signed writings delivered directly to the Agent with evidence of their Bonds. A Direction may be signed in counterparts and is effective when the Required Holders have signed.

**6.3 Counting.** The Agent determines whether the Required Holders have signed by reference to the Register at the record time stated in the Direction, or if none, when the last needed signature is given. Required Holders are counted by Accreted Value, as IV-01 defines them. The Agent may rely on the on-chain Register for each Bond's holder and for the Bond data from which its Accreted Value at the record time is computed, on Clear Lending's computation of those Accreted Values delivered with the Direction request, and on Clear Lending's latest certified list of Excluded Bonds. A Direction signed for a Bond binds any later Holder of that Bond.

**6.4 Duty to Follow.** The Agent will follow a valid Direction, unless it conflicts with law or this Agreement, or would unduly prejudice Holders who did not join it, or would expose the Agent to liability without the assurance Section 6.5 permits.

**6.5 Assurance of Payment.** The Agent may not require indemnity or security from Holders before delivering an Enforcement Notice, making distributions, co-signing or refusing to co-sign under Section 4.5, or acting under Exhibit A. For any other action, including litigation, the Agent may require reasonable assurance of payment of its costs, which its priority in Section 8.2(a) satisfies up to the cap stated there.

**6.6 Acting Without Direction.** Without a Direction, the Agent will act as Article 7 requires and may take any action it reasonably considers necessary to protect the Collateral after an Insolvency Event, as Section 8.7 of IV-01 allows.

## Article 7. Enforcement

**7.1 Notice of Default.** Within two (2) Business Days after the Agent learns of an Event of Default, it will give notice to Clear Lending and post a notice to Holders under Section 8.3.

**7.2 Enforcement Notice.** The Agent will deliver an Enforcement Notice, substantially in the form of Exhibit B, to the BondVault through the Agent Key and to Clear Lending:

(a) within one (1) Business Day after receiving a Direction to do so after an Event of Default; and

(b) at once on learning of an Insolvency Event, to the extent the law allows, without any Direction.

From delivery of an Enforcement Notice, no deployable excess or other asset may leave the BondVault except under Section 7.5, and the BondVault accepts no new purchase or roll.

**7.3 Acceleration.** On a Direction after a Payment Default, the Agent will declare every Bond due at its Accreted Value under Section 11.3 of IV-01. On an Insolvency Event every Bond is due without declaration. The Agent will rescind a declaration on a Direction once every overdue amount is paid.

**7.4 Remedies.** During an Enforcement Period, the Agent may exercise every right of a secured party under Article 9 of the UCC (W.S. 34.1-9-601 and following), W.S. 34-29-103 and other law. Because the Collateral and the Secured Obligations are both in USDC or dollars, the Agent will apply the Collateral directly under Section 7.5 without any disposition, except that on a USDC Disruption, or to pay a Holder in dollars, it may redeem USDC with its issuer, or sell it through a regulated venue at the best price reasonably available, into the Collateral Deposit Account.

**7.5 Application of Collateral.** During an Enforcement Period, the Agent will apply the Collateral and its proceeds, as soon as practicable and in installments if needed, in this order:

*First,* to the Agent's reasonable costs of enforcement and amounts due under Sections 9.2 and 10.3 that Clear Lending has not paid within thirty (30) days after demand, up to the cap in Section 8.2(a);

*Second,* to the Holders of all Series, ratably among Series by the total amount then due on each Series under Section 11.3 of IV-01, and within each Series ratably by the amount due on each Bond, until every Secured Obligation to Holders is paid in full; and

*Third,* any remainder to Clear Lending, or as a court of competent jurisdiction directs.

**7.6 How Holders Are Paid.** The Agent pays each Holder by a distribution from the BondVault to the Holder's Smart Account that holds the Bond, using the distribution function in Exhibit A. It pays a Successor registered under Section 6.3 of IV-01 as the Register records. On a USDC Disruption, or where a Smart Account cannot receive payment, the Agent pays dollars from the Collateral Deposit Account to the Holder's Spendable account or a U.S. bank account in the Holder's name, using details Clear Lending provides under Section 5.2(e) or the Holder gives directly. A payment on a Bond pledged under MB-05 is paid to the Holder's Smart Account and is proceeds of that pledge under MB-05. The Agent records each payment against the Bond, and a Bond paid in full is burned.

**7.7 Withdrawal of an Enforcement Notice.** The Agent will withdraw an Enforcement Notice only on a Direction after every overdue amount has been paid and any declaration rescinded, or if it was delivered in error before any Event of Default.

**7.8 Bankruptcy.** Nothing in this Agreement requires the Agent to act in violation of the automatic stay under 11 U.S.C. § 362. After an Insolvency Event, the Agent will not consent to the use of the Collateral as cash collateral under 11 U.S.C. § 363(c)(2) except on a Direction or a court order, will seek adequate protection and relief from the stay as it considers appropriate or as directed, and may file proofs of claim for the Holders. Any amount the Collateral does not pay remains an unsecured claim of each Holder, ranked under Sections 9.2 and 9.3 of IV-01.

**7.9 Collective Enforcement.** Only the Agent may enforce the security interest, as Section 11.7 of IV-01 provides. Nothing in this Agreement impairs a Holder's right to payment under Section 11.8 of IV-01.

## Article 10. Standard of Care, Exculpation and Indemnity

**10.1 Duties.** The Agent has only the duties expressly stated in this Agreement and IV-01, and no implied duties. During an Enforcement Period, the Agent will exercise its rights and powers with the care and skill a prudent person would use in the conduct of that person's own affairs. The Agent holds the security interest for the Holders as their agent and owes them the duties of an agent in doing so.

**10.2 Exculpation.** The Agent is liable only for its own negligence in holding and using the Agent Key under Section 4.3 (or, if it has made the election in Section 10.5, its gross negligence), and otherwise only for its own gross negligence, bad faith or willful misconduct, as finally determined by a court. The Agent:

(a) may rely in good faith on any certificate, Direction, Wallet Signature, the on-chain Register and BondVault state, and the advice of counsel;

(b) is not responsible for the validity of the Bonds, the sufficiency or value of the Collateral, the correctness of Clear Lending's reports, or the operation of the BondVault's code, the blockchain network or USDC, except to the extent caused by its own failure under this Section;

(c) is not liable for indirect, special or consequential damages; and

(d) is not liable for any action taken or omitted on a Direction.

**10.3 Indemnity.** Clear Lending will indemnify the Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents against every loss, liability, claim and reasonable expense, including counsel fees, arising from this Agreement, except to the extent caused by conduct for which Section 10.2 makes the Agent liable. This indemnity is paid as Section 9.3 provides and survives termination and the Agent's resignation or removal.

**10.4 No Conflict.** The Agent will not act for any Clear Company in any matter adverse to the Holders, and will disclose any lending, custody or other relationship it has with a Clear Company.

**10.5 Key Custody Election.** The Agent may elect, on its signature page when it signs this Agreement or accepts appointment as successor, that its standard of care for holding and using the Agent Key is gross negligence rather than negligence. An Agent that so elects will, from its appointment until its duties end, maintain with insurers rated at least A- by A.M. Best crime, specie or digital-asset custody insurance that covers loss caused by the theft, loss, compromise or unauthorized use of the Agent Key, with a limit of not less than the greater of one million dollars ($1,000,000) and the assets held in the BondVault at the start of each policy year (the "**Custody Insurance**"). It will pay every recovery under the Custody Insurance for loss to the Collateral into the BondVault or, during an Enforcement Period, apply it under Section 7.5, deliver a certificate of the Custody Insurance to Clear Lending at appointment and each renewal, and give Holders and Clear Lending thirty (30) days' notice before it lapses or is reduced. If the Custody Insurance lapses or falls below that limit, the negligence standard applies from that moment. Clear Lending will tell Holders of the election in the next collateral report.

## Article 11. Resignation, Removal and Successor

**11.1 Resignation.** The Agent may resign on sixty (60) days' notice to Clear Lending and Holders, and must resign if it ceases to be eligible under Section 2.4.

**11.2 Removal.** The Required Holders may remove the Agent at any time, with or without cause, by Direction. Clear Lending may remove the Agent, on notice to Holders, if it ceases to be eligible, becomes subject to an insolvency or receivership proceeding, or materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure within thirty (30) days after notice.

**11.3 Successor.** The Required Holders may appoint a successor by Direction. If they do not within thirty (30) days after a notice of resignation or removal, Clear Lending will appoint an eligible successor, and the appointment takes effect unless the Required Holders object within fifteen (15) days after notice of it. If no successor accepts within ninety (90) days, the Agent, Clear Lending or any Holder may petition a court in Wyoming to appoint one.

**11.4 Effect.** No resignation or removal takes effect until an eligible successor that is not an Affiliate of Clear accepts appointment in writing and the Agent Role has passed to the successor's key under Exhibit A, Part 4, as Section 8.7 of IV-01 requires. The retiring Agent will then transfer every Collateral asset it holds and sign every document reasonably needed, including an assignment of the financing statement, and is released from later duties. Article 10 continues to protect it.

**11.5 Merger.** An eligible successor to the Agent by merger, conversion or acquisition of its corporate trust or custody business becomes the Agent without further act.

## Exhibit A. On-Chain Role Specification

This Exhibit states what the Agent Key can and cannot do in the BondVault. Function names are for reference; behavior controls.

**Part 1. Roles in the BondVault.** The BondVault has only these roles: (a) the permissionless settlement and distribution callers described below; (b) Clear Lending's treasury role, held by a two-of-N multisignature account under two-person control, which may propose withdrawals of deployable excess and changes; (c) the BurnerBond token contract, which deposits Purchase Prices and records Bonds; and (d) the Agent Role, held only by the Agent Key.

**Part 2. What the Agent Key can do.**

| # | Power | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Record an Enforcement Notice (`enforce(reasonCode, noticeHash)`) | Callable at any time by the Agent Key; contractually limited to Section 7.2. Takes effect in the same block. |
| 2 | Withdraw an Enforcement Notice (`releaseEnforcement(directionHash)`) | Only as Section 7.7 allows. |
| 3 | Post a distribution round (`postDistribution(amount, roundId)`) | Only during an Enforcement Period. The code computes each Bond's share ratably by its amount due (Accreted Value at the acceleration time, or Maturity Amount if matured) from on-chain Bond data; the Agent sets only the total amount distributed, never any Bond's share. |
| 4 | Transfer USDC to the Collateral Deposit Account (`toCollateralAccount(amount)`) | Only during an Enforcement Period, only to the single address registered for that account, which can be changed only under Part 4. |
| 5 | Withdraw enforcement costs (`withdrawCosts(amount, statementHash)`) | Only during an Enforcement Period, at least seven (7) days after the statement hash is posted on-chain, to the Agent's registered cost address, and never above the running cap in Section 8.2(a), which is stored on-chain. |
| 6 | Co-sign a queued change (`approveChange(changeId)`) | Required for every upgrade, role, parameter or destination change, which also needs Clear Lending's two-person approval and a seven (7) day timelock. |
| 7 | Rotate the Agent Key (`rotateAgentKey(newKey)`) | Signed by the current Agent Key; effective after forty-eight (48) hours. |

**Part 3. What the Agent Key cannot do.** The code makes it impossible for the Agent Key to:

1. transfer USDC to any address other than a Holder's Smart Account through a distribution round, the registered Collateral Deposit Account, or the registered cost address within the cap;
2. set or change any Bond's share of a distribution, Maturity Amount, Issue Yield, Maturity Date or holder;
3. mint, burn, transfer, freeze or pledge any Bond, or touch any Smart Account, CLRUSD, the ESA, the Yield Pool, the PayoutPool, the pledge module or any AssurancePool compartment;
4. withdraw deployable excess, or direct it anywhere;
5. upgrade the BondVault, grant or revoke any role, or change any parameter, on its own;
6. prevent a payment of a Maturity Amount to a Holder on its Maturity Date while no Enforcement Notice is in effect; or
7. withdraw fees, or withdraw costs while no Enforcement Notice is in effect.

**Part 4. Replacement of a lost or compromised Agent Key, and successor Agents.** If the Agent Key cannot sign, a replacement key takes the Agent Role only by a change that (a) the Agent requests in a written instruction authenticated under its signature procedures, (b) Clear Lending approves under two-person control, and (c) passes a seven (7) day timelock with notice to Holders, during which the Required Holders may block it by Direction delivered to the Agent and recorded on-chain. A successor Agent takes the Agent Role by the same procedure, or by rotation signed by the retiring Agent Key. During any replacement, an Enforcement Notice in effect stays in effect.

**Part 5. What Clear Lending's treasury role can do.** Only: (a) withdraw deployable excess to its registered multisignature account under two-person control, and only if the BondVault's assets after the withdrawal are at least the Redemption Reserve and no Enforcement Notice is in effect; and (b) propose changes under Part 2, item 6. Settlement of a matured Bond and a roll are permissionless and pay only to the Holder or into the BondVault. Clear Lending can never move Collateral during an Enforcement Period.

**Part 6. Events.** The BondVault emits an event for every action in Parts 2, 4 and 5, and for every payment per Bond.
