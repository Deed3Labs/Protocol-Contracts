## Enforcement Notice (IV-01)

**11.4 Enforcement Notice.** After an Event of Default, the Collateral Agent, as directed by the Required Holders or on its own after an Insolvency Event, may deliver an enforcement notice to the BondVault and Clear Lending (an "**Enforcement Notice**"), after which no deployable excess may leave the BondVault.

## Successor (IV-01)

**6.3 Transfer by Operation of Law.** On a Holder's death, incapacity, divorce or bankruptcy, a Bond passes to the person the law entitles (the "**Successor**"). Once the Successor gives proof of entitlement sufficient under W.S. 34.1-8-402, completes verification under Article 14 and gives a Form W-9, Clear Lending will register the Successor as Holder and enable the transfer to the Successor's Smart Account, signed from the Holder's Smart Account by the person with authority over it. If that Smart Account cannot be accessed, Clear Lending will instead record the Successor on the Register as entitled to payment, and pay the Successor at maturity after receiving the indemnity W.S. 34.1-8-405 permits for a lost security. A Successor who is not a Member may hold the Bond to maturity, but it will not roll.

## Holdback Percentage (IV-02)

"**Holdback Percentage**" means, while a Pool Loss Allowance above zero is posted, that Pool Loss Allowance divided by Pool Assets, each as last posted under the Valuation Policy, and otherwise zero.

## Redemption Amount (IV-02)

"**Redemption Amount**" means, for any Pool Shares at any time, their number multiplied by the Share Value.

## Accreted Value (IV-01)

"**Accreted Value**" means, for a Bond before its Maturity Date, its Maturity Amount divided by (1 + Issue Yield) raised to the power of the years remaining to the Maturity Date (days remaining divided by three hundred sixty-five (365)), and on and after its Maturity Date, its Maturity Amount. Accreted Value equals Present Value under MB-05.

"**Residual Capital**" means Holdings' contributed capital and retained earnings in Lending Co., held in Liquid Assets, other than amounts in the Loss Reserve.
