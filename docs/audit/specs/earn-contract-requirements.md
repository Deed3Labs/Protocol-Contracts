# Clear Earn — BondVault, BurnerBond and Yield Pool contract requirements (add to the audit fix pass)

Repo: Deed3Labs/Protocol-Contracts, `dev` branch. These are REQUIREMENTS, not suggestions. They come from the Earn offering documents: IV-01 (BurnerBonds), IV-02 (Pool Shares), IV-03 (PPM), IV-10 (Collateral Agency Agreement), L-01, M-02 and MB-05. Apply them alongside the audit fixes and `smart-contract-compliance-requirements.md`, before mainnet. If an item conflicts with an audit finding or that spec, stop and list the conflict instead of choosing silently.

Every rate, ratio and window below is a GOVERNED PARAMETER (timelock, two-person control) unless marked FIXED. FIXED items are legal floors or caps the code must make impossible to cross. Function names are suggestions; behavior is the requirement.

## Background (why)

- The token contracts are the issuer's register of these securities, which are sold in separate Earn offerings under Rule 506(c), Rule 506(b) and, once a California permit issues, Rule 147A (IV-01 §§2.1, 4.4; IV-02 §§3.7, 10.1). The exemption for each sale is recorded off-chain by the app's eligibility step; the only on-chain trace is the tag in section 6. Code that can move or reassign a position outside the documents makes the register and the offering documents wrong.
- BurnerBonds are secured by a first lien on the BondVault, held by an independent bank or trust company as Collateral Agent (IV-10). Only the Redemption Reserve stays in the vault. The lien is perfected by control, so the Agent's role must work exactly as IV-10, Exhibit A says.
- Pool Shares are limited-recourse subordinated demand notes. Their price (Share Value) is principal plus accrued interest less realized loss write-downs; an expected-loss allowance never lowers it and instead sets a Loss Holdback on redemptions. IV-02 promises fair entry and exit (FIFO queue priced at fill, Loss Holdback, limited suspension).

## Do NOT change

No path from the BondVault or the Yield Pool into any AssurancePool compartment, the Loss Reserve or the ESA vault, or the reverse · ESA capital and CLRUSD can never fund Earn (brief, Nov 13 decision) · Earn purchases only from Ready to allocate USDC in the Member's Smart Account · the pledge module stays the only lock on a Member's assets · no Clear key can move or freeze an Earn position in a Member's Smart Account. Do not widen any admin power.

## Required changes

### 1. BurnerBond token (ERC-1155)
- Quantity 1 per token ID; IDs never reused. Store per token: series ID, Maturity Amount, Issue Yield (bps, annual compounding), Issue Date, Maturity Date, opt-out deadline (end of day Pacific before maturity, as a timestamp computed at mint), auto-roll flag (default ON), pledged flag, paid amount.
- `accretedValue(id)` = Maturity Amount ÷ (1 + yield)^(days to maturity ÷ 365), and Maturity Amount at or after maturity. Use fixed-point math with documented rounding; never round in the Holder's disfavor by more than one cent.
- Mint only atomically with receipt of the Purchase Price into the BondVault from the buyer's Smart Account, on a Wallet Signature over a quote that expires after 15 minutes. Purchase Price ≥ $250 (FIXED floor). Reject a purchase that would exceed the Funding Limit or the offering cap (`offeringCap`, initial $1,000,000), or that is made during a Reserve Shortfall or while the Equity Cushion pause is set (rolls excepted in both cases), or while an Enforcement Notice is in effect.
- Reject payment from any Clear contract holding ESA capital, CLRUSD, collateral, credit proceeds or Earn funds.
- Transfer allowlist (FIXED): only (a) burn on settlement, buyback, `payPledged` or `redeemAll`; (b) between two Smart Accounts registered to the same Holder; (c) the Successor path: Clear Lending two-person approval plus an evidence hash, to the Successor's verified Smart Account; (d) pledge to Clear Lending via the pledge module (the token stays in the Holder's account). Every other `safeTransferFrom` reverts. No approvals for third-party operators.
- Metadata carries the IV-01 §4.12 legend.

### 2. BondVault accounting and Redemption Reserve
- Holds only USDC; keeps per-bond and per-series totals.
- `requiredReserve()` = the greater of:
  - Σ over outstanding bonds of `reserveRatio(bond)` × Maturity Amount, where `reserveRatio` follows a glide schedule by time to maturity with a FIXED floor of 30% (`reserveFloorBps = 3000`, can only be raised); and
  - 100% of the Maturity Amount of bonds maturing within 90 days (FIXED window) whose auto-roll is OFF, or that will not roll for another reason the contract can see (holder ineligible flag set, term not offered).
- A late opt-out (auto-roll turned off inside the 90-day window) counts at 100% in the same transaction.
- If assets < `requiredReserve()`: set `shortfallSince`, emit `ReserveShortfall`, block new purchases (not rolls) and all excess withdrawals until `topUp(amount)` cures it. The two-Business-Day cure is tracked off-chain from `shortfallSince`.

### 3. Deployable excess
- `withdrawExcess(amount)` only to the single registered Clear Lending multisig (2-of-N, distinct signers, propose and execute by different addresses), only if assets after the withdrawal ≥ `requiredReserve()`, and only while no Enforcement Notice is in effect. Otherwise revert.
- Changing the destination address is a change under section 7 (timelock plus Agent co-signature).

### 4. Maturity, auto-roll and no early redemption
- `settle(id)` is permissionless at or after the Maturity Date. If auto-roll is OFF (or roll conditions fail), pay the Maturity Amount in USDC to the Holder's Smart Account and burn. If the vault is short, pay what it holds, record the paid amount, and emit `SettlementShortfall`; Clear Lending's later payment completes it.
- Pledged bond that does not roll (MB-05 §5.6 Maturity Instruction; IV-01 §4.5): in the same `settle` call, split the Maturity Amount as follows. `toDebt` = min(Maturity Amount, max(0, Asset-Backed Drawn − `limitWithout`)), where `limitWithout` is the Holder's Asset-Backed Limit computed in that block without this bond (Σ Collateral Value of the Holder's other Pledged Earn Positions at their stored advance rates). `toDebt` is recorded on-chain as a repayment of the Holder's Asset-Backed Drawn (order per MB-04/MB-06 and the compliance spec: carry, then principal), as an application on the Holder's own instruction (MB-05 §7.9), not enforcement; that USDC stays in the BondVault, is no longer owed on the bond, and leaves only as deployable excess under section 3. The rest (`toHolder` = Maturity Amount − `toDebt`) is paid in USDC to the Holder's Smart Account (Ready to allocate). FIXED: no part of `toHolder` is ever locked, held or restricted by the pledge module or any role, and no "Pledged Proceeds" state exists; `toDebt` is computed only from on-chain values in that block, never exceeds the formula, and no role can set, raise or redirect it. The pledge is released and the bond burns in the same transaction. If the vault is short, apply `toDebt` first (it moves no USDC) and apply the shortfall rule above to `toHolder` only. If Asset-Backed Drawn still exceeds `limitWithout` after `toDebt`, the ordinary Shortfall rules of MB-05 apply. Emit `PledgedMaturitySplit(id, toDebt, toHolder)`.
- Roll (IV-01 §5.2): same term or nearest shorter term, at the Roll Notice yield or any higher Ladder yield; new Maturity Amount = old ÷ new price per dollar, rounded down to the cent, remainder to Ready to allocate; a pledged bond's replacement is re-pledged in the same transaction. No USDC leaves the vault on a roll.
- No Holder-callable early redemption. The only early exits: (a) `buyback(id, price, sig)` signed by the Holder after seeing the price, paid from outside the BondVault, blocked during a Reserve Shortfall or Enforcement Notice, price ≤ Accreted Value; (b) `payPledged(id)`, the pledge payment trigger written into the bond (IV-01 §4.9): callable by anyone, but succeeds only when the pledge module reports that MB-05 permits enforcement against that pledged bond (MB-05 §6.1(a): default after notice and cure as recorded on-chain; or §6.1(b): Shortfall uncured for 72 hours) and only for as many bonds as the module requests (MB-05 §7.3(c)). The bond becomes payable at `accretedValue(id)` in that block (FIXED: never less than 100% of Present Value); the amount is applied only to that Holder's MB-05 secured debt in Clear Lending's ledger, in the module's order; any surplus goes in USDC only to the Holder's Smart Account; the bond burns. No other destination, price, caller condition or Clear role can trigger or redirect it, and it never runs for an unpledged or released bond. It is not a buyback and the buyback rules do not apply; (c) `redeemAll()` at Accreted Value for every bond, only after a 30-day timelock and a wind-down or illegality flag set under two-person control.

### 5. Collateral Agent role (IV-10, Exhibit A)
Implement Exhibit A exactly. The Agent Key can:
- `enforce(reasonCode, noticeHash)` at any time: in the same block, stops every outflow except distributions under this section, and stops new purchases and rolls. It never blocks `settle` payments to Holders while no Enforcement Notice is in effect.
- `releaseEnforcement(directionHash)`.
- `postDistribution(amount, roundId)` during enforcement: the contract computes each bond's share ratably by amount due (Accreted Value at the enforcement timestamp, or Maturity Amount if matured), across all series; the Agent sets only the total. Each bond's claim is reduced by what it receives; a bond paid in full is burned. Pull or push payment to the Holder's registered Smart Account only.
- `toCollateralAccount(amount)` during enforcement, only to the registered collateral account.
- `withdrawCosts(amount, statementHash)` during enforcement, at least 7 days after the hash is posted, to the registered cost address, never above the on-chain cap (greater of $50,000 and 2% of assets at enforcement, plus Required-Holder approvals recorded by hash).
- `approveChange(changeId)`: co-signature required for every upgrade, role, parameter or destination change.
- `rotateAgentKey(newKey)` with a 48-hour delay. Lost-key replacement: Agent instruction hash + Clear Lending two-person approval + 7-day timelock, blockable by a recorded Required-Holder direction hash.

The Agent Key can do nothing else (see IV-10 Exhibit A, Part 3).

### 6. Yield Pool vault (ERC-4626)
- Deposits only from eligible Smart Accounts (attestation per IV-02 §2.1) using Ready to allocate USDC; reject the Clear-contract sources in section 1. The existing eligibility attestation carries one added field, `exemptionCode` (a short tag set off-chain by the app's eligibility step, e.g. `506C`, `506B`, `147A`); the vault stores it with the subscription and emits it in the subscription event. No contract logic reads or branches on it. Minimum $250 (FIXED floor); holder cap 10% of shares (`holderCapBps`). Subscriptions pause during a Reserve Shortfall, while the Equity Cushion pause is set (L-01 §8.6A; set and cleared under two-person control like the Reserve Shortfall pause) or during a Suspension; no pause ever affects withdrawals.
- Virtual share offset; round shares issued and assets paid DOWN (IV-02 Exh. A6).
- Share Value (IV-02 §5.5) = Pool Assets ÷ outstanding shares, where Pool Assets = Vault Cash + Deployed Balance + accrued Pool Interest. In substance: principal + accrued interest index − realized loss write-downs. The Pool Loss Allowance is NOT subtracted. Vault Cash excludes Holdback Amounts. Expose `shareValue()` (replacing `navPerShare()`), `holdbackBps()`, `vaultCash()`, `deployedBalance()`, `utilization()`, `fundingRate()`, total and per-Holder Holdback Amounts, queue state and suspension state. ERC-4626 `convertToAssets` uses Share Value; `previewRedeem`, `previewWithdraw`, `maxWithdraw` and `maxRedeem` return amounts net of the Loss Holdback.
- `deploy(amount)`: two-person, only to the registered lending account, only with no open queue request and no Suspension, and only if utilization afterwards ≤ 90% (FIXED cap). `returnFunds(amount)` by anyone.
- Pool Interest accrues per second on Deployed Balance at the Funding Rate (365-day year).
- Impairment hook: `postAllowance(value, evidenceHash)` and `postLossCharge(value, evidenceHash)`: proposed by one authorized individual, approved by a different one; allowance bounded 0 ≤ value ≤ Pool Assets; each posting timestamped. `postAllowance` changes ONLY the Holdback Percentage (`holdbackBps` = allowance ÷ Pool Assets at posting, rounded up) and never the Share Value. A loss charge is applied first to open Holdback Amounts (section 6A), and only the remainder reduces Deployed Balance and, only to the extent it exceeds Deployed Balance plus accrued interest, transfers from the vault to Clear Lending. An error-correction path pays INTO the vault only. No sweep, rescue or any other admin transfer (IV-02 §4.7).
- Interest rate model (IV-02 Sch. 2): if U ≤ K, R = R₀ + S₁·(U/K); else R = R₀ + S₁ + S₂·((U−K)/(1−K)). Initial R₀ 2.00%, S₁ 3.00%, K 80%, S₂ 5.00%. Bounds: R₀ 0–5%, S₁ 0–6%, K 60–90%, S₂ 0–10%, and R₀+S₁+S₂ ≤ 10% (FIXED). A change that does not lower R at any U takes effect after ≥ 48 hours; one that lowers R at any U needs ≥ 30 days' notice and at most one per calendar quarter (store the last such change).
- Transfers (FIXED): same-Holder Smart Accounts, pledge via the pledge module, Successor path (two-person plus evidence hash), burn on redemption. Everything else reverts. Metadata carries the IV-02 §8.4 legend.

### 6A. Loss Holdback (IV-02 §5.6, Exhibit A)
- While `holdbackBps` > 0, every redemption (on demand, `redeemOrQueue`, queue fill, MB-05 enforcement redemption, Successor path, Final Maturity run-off) burns the shares at Share Value (gross amount G), pays G × (1 − `holdbackBps`) (rounded down) to the Holder's Smart Account, and records a holdback for that Holder: {G, held amount H = G − paid, open}. H stays in the vault, is never deployed, earns no interest, and is excluded from Vault Cash, Pool Assets and Utilization. Liquidity checks use the full G.
- Release: when `postAllowance` lowers `holdbackBps`, each open holdback is reduced to min(H, G × new `holdbackBps`) (rounded up) and the excess becomes payable to that Holder's Smart Account only. Processing is permissionless and batched in creation order (`releaseHoldbacks(maxCount)`); a later posting cannot be made, and `postLossCharge` reverts, until releases from the previous lower posting are fully processed. When `holdbackBps` = 0 every holdback is released in full. A higher posting never increases an existing H.
- Maximum age: an open holdback not applied to a loss charge posted within `holdbackMaxAge` (governed; initial 540 days) of its creation is released in full to the Holder's Smart Account. Processing is permissionless and batched in creation order (the same `releaseHoldbacks(maxCount)`); lowering `holdbackMaxAge` applies to existing holdbacks, raising it never does. A loss charge posted before a holdback's age limit still applies to it under the Application rule. While a posted loss charge is pending, age releases wait until its batches complete.
- Application: on `postLossCharge(L)`, each open holdback absorbs min(H, G × L ÷ (Pool Assets before the charge + Σ G of all open holdbacks)) (rounded up). Absorbed amounts move into Vault Cash (Pool Assets); only L − Σ absorbed reduces Deployed Balance. Processing is permissionless and batched (`applyHoldbacks(maxCount)`); until the last batch completes, the charge is pending, and redemptions, queue fills and subscriptions revert (cancellations still allowed). A Holder never loses more than its H.
- No role can choose, raise, redirect or release a holdback amount except by these formulas; released amounts go only to the Holder's registered Smart Account (or the Successor path). Same two-person posting rules as section 6; no new admin power. Emit `HoldbackRecorded(holder, id, gross, held)`, `HoldbackReleased(id, amount)`, `HoldbackApplied(id, amount, lossChargeId)`.
- Pledged shares: the pledge module's value for pledged Pool Shares is `previewRedeem(shares)`, i.e. Share Value less the holdback that would apply now. A holdback created by an MB-05 enforcement redemption is released or applied like any other, to the Holder's Smart Account (its later release is surplus, never re-applied to debt by any role).

### 7. Withdrawals and queue
- `redeem`/`withdraw` succeed on demand only when no queue request is open and vault cash suffices. Standard ERC-4626 `maxWithdraw`/`maxRedeem` must return the true immediately available amount (0 while the queue is open), so integrators never see a call that will revert.
- `redeemOrQueue(shares)`: pays what vault cash allows and records the rest as a queue request in the same transaction ("Take $X now, queue the rest").
- Queue: FIFO by request timestamp and sequence number; no reordering by any role, including Clear Lending as secured party. Queued shares are escrowed in the vault and keep earning and bearing losses. Fill price = Share Value at fill (not at request), less the Loss Holdback then applying (section 6A). `fillQueue(maxCount)` is permissionless, fills in order, whole or partial, up to vault cash. All incoming cash (subscriptions, returns, interest) is applied to the queue first. Owners may cancel all or part of an unfilled request at any time, including during a Suspension.
- Expose the oldest open request's age for the off-chain 30-day and 90-day rules (IV-02 §6.4).
- Pledged shares redeem only as far as MB-05 releases them; enforcement redemptions join the queue in order.

### 8. Suspension
- `suspend(reasonCode, evidenceHash)` with reason codes LOSS_EVENT, LEGAL_ORDER, USDC_OR_NETWORK, VALUATION_PENDING only (FIXED). Set by one authorized individual, approved by a different one, at least one holding the CCO or treasurer role; reason and evidence logged on-chain.
- Auto-expires 72 hours after approval. `renew` must be signed by a signer different from the one who set or last renewed it; same-signer renewal reverts.
- Calendar ceilings that are never longer than the legal Business Day limits: beyond 240 hours a recorded Lending Board resolution hash is required; VALUATION_PENDING ends at 48 hours and cannot be renewed; total for one event ID capped at 30 days except LEGAL_ORDER with an order hash; a new suspension on the same evidence within 30 days needs a resolution hash.
- During a Suspension: no deploy, queue order frozen, cancellations allowed, interest accrues. On end, any pending allowance or loss-charge posting (and its holdback processing) precedes the first fill, and the queue fills first.

### 9. Pledge module integration (MB-05)
- Advance rates are governed parameters stored per pledge at the time of pledging: `advanceRateBondBps` (initial 9500, of Present Value) and `advanceRatePoolBps` (initial 7000, of Pool Share Value), each bounded 0–10000. Decided (founder decision, November 13, 2026): 95% and 70% everywhere (MB-04 §3.3(b), MB-05 §5.1(b)–(c), IV-02 §8.2, L-02 §2.1); the earlier 80% and 60% are superseded.
- Pledging a bond requires the Holder's Wallet Signature over the Maturity Instruction (MB-05 §5.6); store its hash with the pledge. A bond pledge without it reverts.
- Raising a rate takes effect after the timelock. Lowering one needs ≥ 30 days' notice and applies only to pledges made after it; a rate change never by itself creates a Shortfall.
- The module values bonds by `accretedValue(id)` and pool shares by `previewRedeem(shares)` (Share Value less any Loss Holdback then applying; section 6A), and can exit a position only through MB-05 enforcement (`payPledged` for bonds at ≥ Present Value under IV-01 §4.9; queued redemption for pool shares).

### 10. Changes and roles
- BondVault and Yield Pool upgrades: ≥ 7-day timelock, two-person control, Holder notice; BondVault changes also need the Agent's `approveChange`. No upgrade may lower the 30% floor, widen any outflow, remove the Agent role, change the queue order or migrate positions with any change to balances, queue requests or pledges.
- Roles are granted only by Lending Board resolution (hash recorded) within M-02 §2.6.
- Every state change emits an event sufficient to rebuild the Register, queue, Share Value history, holdback ledger and collateral report.

## Acceptance tests (add to the suite)

1. Reserve example (IV-01 §7.3): a bond with Maturity Amount $2,500 bought for $2,267.57 at 30% gives `requiredReserve()` $750.00 and allows a $1,517.57 withdrawal; $1,517.58 reverts.
2. Late opt-out: with a bond maturing in 60 days and auto-roll ON, turning it OFF raises `requiredReserve()` to 100% of its Maturity Amount in the same block, sets `shortfallSince` if under-reserved, and blocks withdrawals and new purchases but not rolls until `topUp`.
3. Withdrawal to any address other than the registered multisig reverts; a withdrawal proposed and executed by the same signer reverts.
4. A $249.99 purchase, a purchase from a CLRUSD, ESA or credit contract, and a quote older than 15 minutes each revert.
5. Every transfer of a bond or pool share outside the allowlist reverts, for every role and for operators.
6. `settle` by a random caller pays the Holder at maturity and burns; before maturity it reverts. A roll keeps all USDC in the vault and computes the new Maturity Amount by the IV-01 §5.2 formula, rounded down, with the remainder paid out.
7. No Holder-callable early redemption exists. A buyback above Accreted Value reverts; a buyback during a Reserve Shortfall or Enforcement Notice reverts.
8. Enforcement: after `enforce`, excess withdrawals, purchases and rolls revert; `postDistribution` of $10,000 across bonds owed $30,000 and $10,000 pays $7,500 and $2,500; the Agent cannot pass per-bond amounts.
9. The Agent Key calling any function to pay a non-Holder, mint, burn, transfer a bond, withdraw excess, or upgrade alone reverts. `withdrawCosts` before 7 days, above the cap, or with no Enforcement Notice reverts.
10. A BondVault upgrade without `approveChange`, or before 7 days, reverts; while no Enforcement Notice is in effect, no Agent action can stop `settle` paying a matured bond.
11. Funding Rate at initial parameters: U = 0% → 2.00%; 50% → 3.875%; 80% → 5.00%; 90% → 7.50%; 100% → 10.00%. A parameter set with R₀+S₁+S₂ > 10% reverts. A rate-lowering change before 30 days, or a second one in the same quarter, reverts.
12. `deploy` above 90% utilization, or with an open queue request or a Suspension, reverts.
13. Queue: requests A then B; B cannot fill before A; a Pool Loss Charge lowers Share Value 2% between request and fill and A is filled at the new Share Value; A cancels half before fill and gets the rest; `maxRedeem` returns 0 while the queue is open. With `holdbackBps` = 150 at fill, A receives 98.5% of its gross fill and a holdback of 1.5% is recorded to A.
14. Suspension: setting and approving by the same signer reverts; it lifts at 72 hours; same-signer renewal reverts, different-signer renewal succeeds; beyond 240 hours without a resolution hash it reverts; VALUATION_PENDING cannot be renewed; a reason code outside the four reverts.
15. `postAllowance` above Pool Assets reverts; posting by a single signer reverts; a valid `postAllowance` leaves `shareValue()` unchanged to the wei and sets `holdbackBps`; a Pool Loss Charge is applied to open holdbacks first and transfers out of the vault only the part of the remainder above Deployed Balance plus accrued interest.
16. Pledge: lowering `advanceRatePoolBps` from 7000 to 6000 leaves existing pledges at 7000 and creates no Shortfall; a pledged bond's roll re-pledges the new bond in the same transaction.
17. No path in or out: fuzz every BondVault, token, Yield Pool and pledge-module function with any inputs; every AssurancePool compartment, Loss Reserve and ESA vault balance is unchanged, and no function on those contracts can send to the BondVault or Yield Pool.
18. Pledge payment trigger: `payPledged` on an unpledged bond, a pledged bond with no recorded enforcement condition, a Shortfall under 72 hours, or a default before the recorded cure date reverts. On a qualifying default with debt $500 against a bond with Accreted Value $950, $500 is applied to the debt, $450 goes to the Holder's Smart Account and the bond burns; no call can pay less than `accretedValue(id)` or send any amount elsewhere.
19. Pledged maturity split (MB-05 §5.6): a pledged bond with Maturity Amount $1,000 (advance rate 9500) and a second pledged bond with Present Value $500 (Collateral Value $475); Asset-Backed Drawn $1,200; auto-roll OFF. `settle` by a random caller applies $725 to Asset-Backed Drawn (leaving $475, within the remaining limit), pays $275 in USDC to the Holder's Smart Account with no lock, releases the pledge, burns the bond and emits `PledgedMaturitySplit(id, 725, 275)`. With Asset-Backed Drawn $400 instead, `toDebt` is $0 and the Holder receives $1,000. With Asset-Backed Drawn $1,600, `toDebt` is $1,000 and the remaining $125 above the limit is left to the Shortfall rules. With the vault short, the $725 is still applied and only the Holder's $275 is subject to `SettlementShortfall`. No role or caller can change `toDebt` or send it anywhere but the Holder's Asset-Backed Drawn, and no function can lock any part of the Holder's payment.

20. Loss Holdback worked example (IV-02 Exh. A10): Pool Assets $100,000, Share Value $1.04, allowance $1,500 → `holdbackBps` 150. Redeeming 10,000 shares pays $10,244 and records a holdback {G $10,400, H $156}; Vault Cash, Pool Assets and Utilization exclude the $156. Posting `holdbackBps` 50 lets `releaseHoldbacks` pay $104 to the Holder's Smart Account (H $52). `postLossCharge($900)` with Pool Assets $89,600 and this as the only open holdback applies $52 (ratable $93.60, capped), adds $52 to Vault Cash and reduces Deployed Balance by $848. Rounding never favors the leaver.
21. Holdback invariants: no role can call any function that pays a holdback to an address other than the Holder's registered Smart Account (or the Successor path), raises an existing H, or applies more than the formula; a higher allowance posting leaves every existing H unchanged; with `holdbackBps` = 0, `previewRedeem` equals shares × `shareValue()` and every open holdback is releasable in full; `postLossCharge` reverts while releases from an earlier lower posting are unprocessed; redemptions, fills and subscriptions revert while a posted loss charge has unapplied holdbacks, and cancellations still succeed.
22. Interest and price: with no loss charge, `shareValue()` rises monotonically as Pool Interest accrues, whatever the allowance; a subscription made while `holdbackBps` > 0 mints at `shareValue()` with nothing deducted.
23. Pledge valuation: with `holdbackBps` = 150, the pledge module values 1,000 pledged shares at `previewRedeem(1000)` = 98.5% of 1,000 × `shareValue()`; an MB-05 enforcement redemption records a holdback to the pledgor, and its later release goes only to the pledgor's Smart Account.
24. Exemption tag and Equity Cushion pause: a subscription stores and emits the `exemptionCode` from its eligibility attestation, and changing the tag changes no other outcome of the call; while the Equity Cushion pause is set, Yield Pool subscriptions and new BurnerBond purchases revert, rolls and all withdrawals succeed; setting or clearing the pause by a single signer reverts.
25. Holdback age: with `holdbackMaxAge` = 540 days and `holdbackBps` unchanged at 150, a holdback created on day 0 cannot be released before day 540 and is releasable in full by any caller from day 540, paid only to the Holder's Smart Account; a loss charge posted on day 539 applies to it under 6A; raising `holdbackMaxAge` to 720 on day 500 leaves its release date at day 540.
