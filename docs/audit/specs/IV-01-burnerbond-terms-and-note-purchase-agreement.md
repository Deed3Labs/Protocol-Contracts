## Article 1. Definitions

**1.1 Adopted Terms.** Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Cooperative Operating Agreement of Clear Collective LLC (C-01). Any other capitalized term has the meaning given in the first of these that defines it: L-01 (including "Bond Class", "Funding Limit", "Issuance", "Lending Board", "Pool Class", "Reserve Shortfall" and "Residual Capital"); M-02 (including "BondVault", "Liquid Assets", "Loss Reserve", "Maturity Amount", "Redemption Reserve" and "Spread"); I-11; MB-03; MB-05 (including "Present Value"); MB-17 (including "Wallet Signature"); and IV-03 (including "Earn Offering", "Exemption Code", "Intrastate Offering", "Investor Questionnaire", "Offering Period", "Rule 506(b) Offering", "Rule 506(c) Offering", "Verification" and "Verified Accredited Investor").

**1.2 Defined Terms.**

"**Accreted Value**" means, for a Bond before its Maturity Date, its Maturity Amount divided by (1 + Issue Yield) raised to the power of the years remaining to the Maturity Date (days remaining divided by three hundred sixty-five (365)), and on and after its Maturity Date, its Maturity Amount. Accreted Value equals Present Value under MB-05.

"**Bond**" or "**BurnerBond**" means a zero-coupon note of Clear Lending issued under this Agreement.

"**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in Wyoming or California may close.

"**Holder**" means the owner of a Bond shown on the Register, including you.

"**Issue Date**" means the date a Bond is minted, and "**Issue Yield**" the annual yield, compounded annually, fixed for a Bond when it is priced.

"**Ladder**" means the schedule of terms, Issue Yields and prices published under Section 3.3, in the form of Schedule 2.

"**Maturity Date**" means the date that is the term of a Bond, in months, after its Issue Date.

"**Purchase Price**" means the amount paid for a Bond.

"**Required Holders**" means Holders of more than fifty percent (50%) of the aggregate Accreted Value of the Bonds outstanding, or of the Series this Agreement names, measured at the record date or record time of the consent or direction concerned, excluding Bonds held by Clear Lending, Holdings, Clear Capital Management LLC, their Affiliates, and the members of the Lending Board and executive officers of Clear Lending.

"**Series**" means all Bonds with the same Issue Date and Maturity Date. Each Series is an Issuance and a Bond Class under L-01.

"**USDC**" means USD Coin issued by Circle Internet Financial, LLC or its successor.

## Article 3. Purchase of Bonds

**3.1 Acceptance.** You accept this Agreement in the Clear app. Your acceptance in the app is your signature. Each Bond you buy is bought under this Agreement, and you sign each purchase with a Wallet Signature.

**3.2 Pricing Policy.** The Lending Board, with its Independent Manager's vote, approves the written pricing policy under which it sets the Issue Yield for each term on the Ladder (the "**Pricing Policy**"). No minimum or maximum Issue Yield is fixed by this Agreement, but every Issue Yield is always below any usury or charge limit that applies to the Bond, and the Issue Yield, price and Maturity Date are disclosed on the Ladder before each purchase or roll. Clear Lending applies the Pricing Policy the same way to every Member at the same time. No Member, Affiliate or insider gets a better price than the Ladder shows.

**3.3 The Ladder.** During an Offering Period, Clear Lending publishes the Ladder on the Earn page. For each term then offered, it shows the Issue Yield, the price per one thousand dollars ($1,000) of Maturity Amount, and the Maturity Date for a purchase made that day. Terms are offered from twelve (12) months to thirty-six (36) months until Phase 3 under Part 8 of the Credit Policy of Clear Lending (L-02) begins, and from twelve (12) months to sixty (60) months after that. Clear Lending may update the Ladder, withdraw any term or suspend sales at any time, under the Pricing Policy; an update applies only to purchases and rolls priced after it. Each Ladder entry, read with Schedule 1, is the term sheet of each Series issued from it.

**3.4 Price.** The price of a Bond per one thousand dollars ($1,000) of Maturity Amount is one thousand dollars ($1,000) divided by (1 + Issue Yield) raised to the power of the term in years (days from the Issue Date to the Maturity Date divided by three hundred sixty-five (365)).

**3.5 How You Buy.** You choose a term, and either the Maturity Amount you want or the amount you want to pay; the other is computed under Section 3.4 and rounded to the nearest cent. The purchase screen shows the Purchase Price, Maturity Amount, Issue Yield, Maturity Date, dollar gain, tax summary and that the Bond will be paid at maturity unless you confirm a roll under Article 5. A price shown lapses after fifteen (15) minutes. The Purchase Price of each Bond must be at least two hundred fifty dollars ($250).

**3.6 Payment Only From Ready to Allocate.** The Purchase Price is paid only in USDC from your Ready to allocate balance, never from ESA capital or CLRUSD or with the proceeds of credit. Your signed purchase moves the Purchase Price to the BondVault and mints the Bond to your Smart Account in one transaction; if either step fails, neither occurs.

**3.7 Clear Lending's Acceptance.** A purchase is accepted only when the Bond is minted. Clear Lending may decline any purchase, and will decline one that would exceed the Funding Limit or the IV-03 maximum, that is made during a Reserve Shortfall or while the Equity Cushion under Section 8.6A of L-01 is not met (other than a roll in either case), that is made while Section 10.7 applies, or that Article 2, Article 14 or the law does not permit.

**3.8 Confirmation.** Clear Lending delivers a confirmation in the form of Schedule 3 within one (1) Business Day after each purchase or roll.

## Article 4. Terms of the Bonds

**4.1 Promise to Pay.** For value received, Clear Lending promises to pay the Holder of each Bond its Maturity Amount on its Maturity Date. Each Bond is a senior debt obligation of Clear Lending, secured as Article 8 provides. No interest accrues other than the accretion of the discount. A Maturity Amount is never reduced, deferred or written down for any credit loss.

**4.2 Form.** Each Bond is an ERC-1155 token of quantity one (1) with a unique token identifier, on the contract listed in the Completion Checklist. Bonds are not fungible with one another. Each token records its Series, Maturity Amount, Issue Yield, Issue Date and Maturity Date.

**4.3 Securities Governed by Article 8.** Each Bond expressly provides, and Clear Lending and each Holder agree, that it is a security governed by Article 8 of the Uniform Commercial Code as in effect in Wyoming (W.S. 34.1-8-101 and following), and a digital security under W.S. 34-29-102. Each Bond is an uncertificated security. Wyoming is the issuer's jurisdiction under W.S. 34.1-8-110. No certificate will be issued.

**4.4 The Register.** The on-chain record of the BurnerBond token contract, as Clear Lending maintains it, is Clear Lending's register of Bonds and registered owners (the "**Register**") for all purposes, including Article 8. Its off-chain record linking each Smart Account to a Member is used only for payment, tax reporting and compliance. The on-chain record controls, except as Section 6.3 or a court order provides.

**4.5 Payment at Maturity.** On the Maturity Date, unless the Bond rolls under Article 5, a settlement function anyone may call pays the Maturity Amount in USDC from the BondVault to the Holder's Smart Account (Ready to allocate), or for a Bond pledged under MB-05 as the Holder's Maturity Instruction under Section 5.6 of MB-05 directs, and burns the Bond, as you authorize when you buy. If the BondVault does not hold enough, Clear Lending pays the rest from its other assets at Step 2 of Section 9.2 of L-01.

**4.6 Dollars, Paid in USDC.** Each Bond is denominated in U.S. dollars and paid by delivering one (1) USDC for each dollar owed. If USDC cannot be delivered, has traded below ninety-nine cents ($0.99) for twenty-four (24) hours, or its issuer has suspended redemptions (a "**USDC Disruption**"), Clear Lending will instead pay dollars to the Holder's Spendable account or a U.S. bank account in the Holder's name. Clear Lending bears that risk; a Holder never receives less than the dollars owed.

**4.7 No Early Redemption Right.** No Holder may require Clear Lending to redeem or repurchase a Bond before its Maturity Date. A Bond is paid or redeemed before its Maturity Date only under Sections 4.8 to 4.10.

**4.8 Discretionary Buyback.** A Holder may offer a Bond to Clear Lending in the app. Clear Lending may accept or decline at its discretion, under a buyback policy approved by the Lending Board and applied the same way to every Holder, which may give priority to death, disability or documented hardship. The price is the Maturity Amount discounted to the buyback date at the higher of the Issue Yield and the Ladder yield then offered for the nearest term, so it never exceeds Accreted Value. A buyback is completed only on the Holder's Wallet Signature after the price is shown; is paid from Liquid Assets outside the BondVault, and only if every requirement of Article 8 of L-01 is met after it; and is not made while a Reserve Shortfall or Event of Default exists or while Clear Lending knows material information about itself that Holders do not have. A Bond bought back is cancelled.

**4.9 Pledged Bonds Payable on the Pledgor's Default.** This Section is a term of every Bond.

(a) *When payable.* A Bond pledged under the Security and Pledge Agreement (MB-05) becomes due and payable by Clear Lending, without any act of the Holder, when MB-05 permits enforcement against it under MB-05 Section 6.1(a) (a default of the Holder's secured credit, after the notice and cure period MB-05 requires) or MB-05 Section 6.1(b) (a Shortfall that has continued uncured for the period in MB-05 Section 6.5), in each case as recorded on-chain, and only for as many pledged Bonds as MB-05 Section 7.3(c) permits.

(b) *Amount.* The amount payable is not less than one hundred percent (100%) of the Bond's Present Value (which equals its Accreted Value) at the time of payment (the "**Pledge Payment Amount**").

(c) *Payable only to the secured debt.* Clear Lending pays the Pledge Payment Amount only by applying it to the Holder's obligations secured under MB-05, in the order MB-05 Article 7 sets, and pays any surplus in USDC to the Holder's Smart Account (Ready to allocate) within two (2) Business Days. It may not pay the Pledge Payment Amount to any other person, apply it to any other obligation, or pay less. The Bond is then cancelled.

(d) *Nature.* A payment under this Section is payment of the Bond by its terms, not a buyback, sale or other disposition. No Holder may require payment under this Section, and it does not apply to a Bond that is not pledged, or once MB-05 has released the pledge.

**4.10 Wind-Down or Regulatory Redemption.** Clear Lending may redeem all, and not some, outstanding Bonds at Accreted Value, on at least thirty (30) days' notice, only while winding up under Article 17 of L-01 with enough to pay every Bond in full, or if counsel advises in writing that keeping Bonds outstanding has become unlawful.

**4.11 No Set-Off.** Clear Lending will not set off a Bond against anything a Holder owes a Clear Company, except by paying a pledged Bond under Section 4.9.

**4.12 Legend.** The Register, each confirmation and the token metadata carry this legend: "This BurnerBond has not been registered under the Securities Act of 1933 or any state securities law. It may not be transferred except to Clear Lending Co. or by operation of law, as the BurnerBond Terms (IV-01) provide."

## Article 5. Rolls

**5.1 Each Roll Needs Your Confirmation.** A Bond rolls at maturity only if you confirm that roll by Wallet Signature in the app on its Roll Notice (a "**Roll Confirmation**"), after the Roll Notice is delivered and before the end of the day (Pacific Time) on the fifth (5th) Business Day before its Maturity Date (the "**Roll Deadline**"). Each Roll Confirmation covers one roll of one Bond. No standing instruction, earlier confirmation or setting in the app rolls a Bond; a roll preference you set in the app only pre-selects the choice shown on the Roll Notice. You may withdraw a Roll Confirmation in the app at any time before the Roll Deadline. After the Roll Deadline, a Roll Confirmation can be neither given nor withdrawn. If no Roll Confirmation is in effect at the Roll Deadline, the Bond does not roll and is paid on its Maturity Date under Section 4.5.

**5.2 What a Roll Is.** A roll is a new purchase of a new Bond. On the Maturity Date, under your Roll Confirmation, the Maturity Amount of the maturing Bond pays the Purchase Price of a new Bond that:

(a) has the same term or, if that term is not then offered, the nearest shorter term on the Ladder;

(b) has the Issue Yield stated in the Roll Notice, or any higher Issue Yield the Ladder shows for that term on the Maturity Date;

(c) has a Maturity Amount equal to the old Maturity Amount divided by the new price per dollar of Maturity Amount, rounded down to the cent, with any remainder paid to your Ready to allocate; and

(d) if the maturing Bond was pledged under MB-05, is pledged in its place with no gap.

The new Bond is governed by this Agreement as then in effect, and its Issue Yield may be lower. It is sold in the Earn Offering for which you then qualify under Section 2.2, and its Exemption Code is recorded under Section 2.1. A Bond sold in the Rule 506(b) Offering that matures while that offering is closed rolls only into the Rule 506(c) Offering, and only if you are then a Verified Accredited Investor whose Verification is not more than five (5) years old; otherwise it does not roll. The new Bond rolls at its own maturity only on a new Roll Confirmation under Section 5.1.

**5.3 Roll Notice.** At least twenty (20) days before each Maturity Date, Clear Lending will send a notice in the app and by email (a "**Roll Notice**") stating the new term, Issue Yield, price and Maturity Date; the Roll Deadline; that the Bond will be paid to your Ready to allocate at maturity unless you give a Roll Confirmation by the Roll Deadline; any supplement to IV-03 or change to this Agreement since your last purchase; and how to give or withdraw a Roll Confirmation. If you have not given a Roll Confirmation, Clear Lending will send a reminder in the app and by email at least two (2) Business Days before the Roll Deadline.

**5.4 When No Roll Occurs.** A Bond is paid under Section 4.5 instead of rolling if no Roll Confirmation is in effect at the Roll Deadline; no term of the same length or shorter is offered; the Holder is not then eligible under Article 2 or Article 14, or is a Successor who is not a Member; the Holder has not confirmed the Investor Questionnaire within the twelve (12) months before the Maturity Date; in the Rule 506(c) Offering, the Holder's Verification is more than five (5) years old on the Maturity Date or Clear Lending is aware of information contrary to the Holder's accredited status; the Roll Notice was late; Section 10.7 applies on the Maturity Date; or the roll would exceed the Funding Limit or the IV-03 maximum, or breach the law.

**5.5 Your Investment Decision.** Each Roll Confirmation is your decision to buy the new Bond and your written representation, made when you sign it and repeated as of the Maturity Date, of each matter in Article 15, including, in the Rule 506(c) Offering, that you remain an accredited investor. If any of them becomes untrue before the Maturity Date, you must withdraw the Roll Confirmation before the Roll Deadline or, after it, tell Clear Lending in the app and update the Investor Questionnaire, and the Bond is then paid under Section 4.5 instead of rolling.

## Article 6. Transfers and Pledges

**6.1 Transfers Prohibited.** A Holder may not sell, give, assign or otherwise transfer a Bond or any interest in it, except (a) to Clear Lending under Sections 4.8 to 4.10, (b) by operation of law under Section 6.3, or (c) by pledge to Clear Lending under MB-05. Any other purported transfer is void. There is no secondary market for the Bonds, and Clear Lending will not create, support or recognize one.

**6.2 Enforced by Code.** The BurnerBond token contract rejects every transfer Section 6.1 does not permit, other than a move between two Smart Accounts registered to the same Holder, such as on a wallet replacement.

**6.3 Transfer by Operation of Law.** On a Holder's death, incapacity, divorce or bankruptcy, a Bond passes to the person the law entitles (the "**Successor**"). Once the Successor gives proof of entitlement sufficient under W.S. 34.1-8-402, completes verification under Article 14 and gives a Form W-9, Clear Lending will register the Successor as Holder and enable the transfer to the Successor's Smart Account, signed from the Holder's Smart Account by the person with authority over it. If that Smart Account cannot be accessed, Clear Lending will instead record the Successor on the Register as entitled to payment, and pay the Successor at maturity after receiving the indemnity W.S. 34.1-8-405 permits for a lost security. A Successor who is not a Member may hold the Bond to maturity, but it will not roll.

**6.4 Pledge Under MB-05.** A Holder may pledge Bonds only to Clear Lending, as collateral under MB-05. Clear Lending, as issuer, agrees with each Holder who pledges a Bond that it will comply with instructions originated by Clear Lending, as secured party under MB-05, about the pledged Bond without the Holder's further consent, under Uniform Commercial Code Section 8-106(c), and the Holder consents to that agreement. A pledged Bond stays in the Holder's Smart Account under the pledge module. MB-05 governs the collateral value, enforcement, rolls and release of a pledged Bond, and controls if it conflicts with this Agreement on those matters.

**6.5 Clear Lending's Powers Over Your Bonds.** Clear Lending holds no key, role or session key that can move a Bond out of a Holder's Smart Account or freeze it. Its only powers over a Bond are settlement and rolls under Articles 4 and 5, a buyback the Holder signs, a payment under Section 4.9, a redemption under Section 4.10, registration of a Successor under Section 6.3, and a hold the law requires under Section 14.2.

**6.6 No Payments From Bonds.** Bonds are not a means of payment. No card transaction, Send under MB-09, bill payment, ACH debit or automatic clearing under MB-04 is ever paid from a Bond or from the BondVault. A Bond is paid only under Articles 4 and 5, into the Holder's Ready to allocate (or as Section 4.6 or 4.9 provides). Clear Lending does not describe Bonds, in the app or elsewhere, as a deposit, an account, savings, a certificate of deposit or an interest-bearing account, and does not state any Issue Yield as an annual percentage yield.

## Article 7. BondVault, Redemption Reserve and Use of Proceeds

**7.1 Proceeds to the BondVault.** The Purchase Price of every Bond, including on a roll, is paid into the BondVault, at the address in the Completion Checklist, which Clear Lending owns. It holds only USDC, accounts for each Bond and holds the Redemption Reserve.

**7.2 Redemption Reserve.** The Redemption Reserve will at all times be at least the greater of:

(a) the reserve ratio in the BondVault parameters, applied to the Maturity Amount of each outstanding Bond, which is initially thirty percent (30%), rises for each Bond as its maturity approaches under the schedule in those parameters, and may never be below thirty percent (30%); and

(b) one hundred percent (100%) of the Maturity Amount of Bonds maturing within the next ninety (90) days, other than Bonds with a Roll Confirmation in effect that will roll.

A Bond whose Roll Confirmation is withdrawn, or that will not roll under Section 5.4 or 5.5 after a Roll Confirmation, counts under clause (b) from that moment. Clear Lending will restore the Redemption Reserve within two (2) Business Days after any shortfall.

**7.3 Deployable Excess.** Only the amount by which the BondVault's assets exceed the Redemption Reserve (the "**deployable excess**") may leave the BondVault, and only to Clear Lending's own multisignature account at the address in the Completion Checklist, under two-person control. The BondVault enforces this in code: a withdrawal that would leave the Redemption Reserve below Section 7.2 fails. For example, for a Bond with a Maturity Amount of two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500) bought for two thousand two hundred sixty-seven dollars and fifty-seven cents ($2,267.57), the reserve at issue is seven hundred fifty dollars ($750) and the deployable excess is one thousand five hundred seventeen dollars and fifty-seven cents ($1,517.57).

**7.4 Use of Deployable Excess.** Deployable excess is used only for the purposes Section 5.2 of IV-03 names, and is held in Liquid Assets until used. It never funds credit under I-06, including any Interim Tranche, or the special-purpose lender wholly owned by Holdings under I-06 (which Clear Lending never funds or guarantees), or any loan secured by Land, and never supports any obligation of Clear Properties Co. or any housing entity.

**7.5 Never in the AssurancePool.** No Bond proceeds, principal, accreted value or Redemption Reserve ever enters, or counts toward, Clear's AssurancePool, Clear Lending's Loss Reserve or RTD, and no Bond is paid from either. No ESA capital, CLRUSD or Member collateral is ever paid into the BondVault.

## Article 8. Security and the Collateral Agent

**8.1 Collateral Agent.** [●] (the "**Collateral Agent**"), a bank or trust company that is not an Affiliate of Clear, acts for the Holders of all Series under its collateral agency and control agreement with Clear Lending dated [●] (the "**Collateral Agency Agreement**"). Each Holder, by buying a Bond, appoints it as agent for this Article and Article 11. Clear Lending pays its fees as Operating Expenses.

This is the security interest Section 4.4 of L-01 requires.

**8.3 Perfection.** The security interest is perfected by a financing statement filed with the Wyoming Secretary of State (filing number [●]) and by control: the BondVault gives the Collateral Agent a role that, on an Enforcement Notice, stops every withdrawal of deployable excess and permits payment only to Holders under Section 11.5. **8.5 Ratable Sharing.** The Collateral secures all Series equally and ratably by Accreted Value, with no priority among them.

**8.6 No Other Lien.** Clear Lending will not grant anyone else a lien on or control over the Collateral, which is never available to any other creditor, the Pool Class or Holdings until every Bond is paid in full.

**8.7 Collateral Agent's Duties.** The Collateral Agent has only the duties stated in this Agreement and the Collateral Agency Agreement, and acts on the written direction of the Required Holders, except that after an Insolvency Event it may act on its own to protect the Collateral. It may resign, or be removed by the Required Holders, only when a successor that is not an Affiliate of Clear accepts appointment.

## Article 9. Ranking and Loss Absorption

**9.1 Senior to Loss Absorption.** Credit losses are absorbed in the order in Section 10.1 of L-01: the Spread for the period; the whole of Clear Lending's own Loss Reserve; for charged-off principal only, draws from the Credit Backstop Compartment of Clear's AssurancePool under I-11, up to Available Coverage; Holdings' Residual Capital; and then the Pool Class, by Pool Loss Charges that reduce the Redemption Amount of Yield Pool shares (IV-02). No credit loss is ever allocated to a Bond.

**9.2 When Holders Can Lose Money.** Holders bear a loss only if every layer in Section 9.1 has been exhausted, the Collateral has been applied to the Bonds, and Clear Lending's remaining assets cannot pay a Maturity Amount when due. Holders are then unpaid creditors for the shortfall, ratably among all Series by Accreted Value. ## Article 11. Events of Default and Remedies

**11.1 Events of Default.** Each of these is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) *Payment Default.* Clear Lending fails to pay the Maturity Amount of a Bond, or to complete a roll the Holder confirmed under Section 5.1, on its Maturity Date, and the failure continues for five (5) Business Days.

(b) *Insolvency Event.* Clear Lending files a voluntary bankruptcy or insolvency petition; an involuntary petition against it leads to an order for relief or is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; a receiver, custodian, conservator or similar official, including one appointed by a regulator, takes charge of it or a substantial part of its assets and is not discharged within sixty (60) days; it makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors; or it admits in writing that it cannot pay its debts as they come due (each an "**Insolvency Event**").

**11.2 Notice.** Clear Lending will notify every Holder and the Collateral Agent within one (1) Business Day after an Event of Default.

**11.3 Acceleration.** On an Insolvency Event, every Bond becomes due at once, without notice, at its Accreted Value on that date. After a Payment Default, the Collateral Agent, at the direction of the Required Holders, may declare every Bond due at once at its Accreted Value on that date, and the Required Holders may rescind that declaration once every overdue amount is paid. A matured Bond is always due at its Maturity Amount.

**11.4 Enforcement Notice.** After an Event of Default, the Collateral Agent, as directed by the Required Holders or on its own after an Insolvency Event, may deliver an enforcement notice to the BondVault and Clear Lending (an "**Enforcement Notice**"), after which no deployable excess may leave the BondVault.

**11.5 Application of Collateral.** The Collateral Agent applies the Collateral and its proceeds: first, to its reasonable costs of enforcement not paid by Clear Lending; second, to the Holders of all Series, ratably by the amounts then due under Section 11.3, until paid in full; and last, any remainder to Clear Lending. Payments go to each Holder's Smart Account in USDC, or as Section 4.6 provides on a USDC Disruption.

**11.6 Deficiency and Bankruptcy.** Any amount the Collateral does not pay remains an unsecured claim, ranked under Sections 9.2 and 9.3. The Bankruptcy Code may stay enforcement, and in a bankruptcy case a Holder's claim is generally limited to Accreted Value at the petition date.

**11.7 Collective Enforcement.** Only the Collateral Agent may enforce the security interest. No Holder may start an insolvency proceeding against Clear Lending, or enforce Article 8, unless the Collateral Agent has not acted within thirty (30) days after the Required Holders directed it to.

**11.8 Your Right to Be Paid.** Each Holder has the absolute and unconditional right to receive the Maturity Amount of its Bond on its Maturity Date, to sue for it once due, and to enforce for its own Bonds the provisions of L-01 named in Section 9.3. That right cannot be impaired without that Holder's consent.
