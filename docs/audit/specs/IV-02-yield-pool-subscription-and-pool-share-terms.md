## Article 2. Eligibility and Subscription

**2.1 Who May Subscribe.** You may subscribe only if, at the time of each subscription:

(a) you are a natural person at least eighteen (18) years old and a Voting Member who has completed Activation;

(b) you qualify for an Earn Offering then open under Section 10.2, and Clear Lending has recorded its Exemption Code for the subscription;

(c) you reside in California, or in another state opened by a supplement to IV-03 after Clear Lending made that state's notice filing;

(d) you are a United States person under Section 7701(a)(30) of the Internal Revenue Code and have certified your taxpayer identification number on Form W-9 in the App;

(e) your identity has been verified and screened under Section 10.6;

(f) you have completed the Investor Questionnaire and confirmed it within the last twelve (12) months;

(g) you have received the current IV-03 and its supplements; and

(h) you subscribe for your own account through your own Smart Account, not through a retirement account, trust, benefit plan or entity.

**2.2 Launch Conditions.** No Pool Share will be issued until every condition to the first sale in IV-03 is met, including designation of Clear's Second Authorized Signer (F-05, Section 6.3) and an independent audit of the Vault and the Pledge Module.

**2.3 How You Subscribe.** During an open Offering Period, you sign for an amount of USDC on the Earn page by Wallet Signature. The Earn page shows the Share Value and the Holdback Percentage before you sign. In one transaction, the Vault takes that USDC from your Smart Account and issues Pool Shares to it at the Share Value (one dollar ($1.00) at the Initial Closing), rounded down, with nothing deducted. While the Holdback Percentage is above zero, Pool Shares are still issued at the full Share Value, which the Pool Loss Allowance does not reduce, and they bear their ratable share of every Pool Loss Charge posted after they are issued, including for losses that were already expected when you subscribed. Whenever the Holdback Percentage is above zero, the Earn page shows, next to it and before you sign: "Losses are expected in the pool. You buy at the full Share Value, with no discount for them. If they are realized later, your Pool Shares bear their share, as every other Holder's do."

**2.4 Source of Funds.** You pay only in USDC from Ready to allocate, which you may first fund from Spendable under Article 6 of MB-02. You may never pay with ESA capital, CLRUSD, Member collateral or the proceeds of credit from Clear Lending or any other lender. The Vault rejects payment from any Clear contract holding ESA capital, collateral, credit or Earn funds.

**2.5 Limits.** A subscription is accepted only if, after it:

(a) it is at least two hundred fifty dollars ($250);

(b) your Earn Products, at their purchase price, total no more than ten percent (10%) of your net worth as Section 2.5 of IV-03 establishes it;

(c) you, with your Immediate Family in your household, hold no more than ten percent (10%) of the outstanding Pool Shares;

(d) Pool Assets are within the pool capacity the Lending Board sets, Clear Lending is within the Funding Limit, and sales are within the maximum in IV-03.

Clear Lending will ask for supporting documents if anything it knows conflicts with your Investor Questionnaire.

**2.6 Acceptance and Pauses.** A subscription is accepted only when Pool Shares are issued; otherwise your USDC stays in your Smart Account. Clear Lending may decline any subscription or pause subscriptions. It will pause them during a Reserve Shortfall (Section 8.9(e) of L-01) and while the Equity Cushion is not met (Section 8.6A of L-01). While Section 2.9(c) of L-01 applies, it will also pause them once Utilization has stayed below fifty percent (50%) for thirty (30) consecutive days, until Utilization is again at least fifty percent (50%). A pause never affects redemptions. Each subscription is confirmed in writing within one (1) Business Day.

## Article 3. What a Pool Share Is

**3.1 Promise to Pay.** For value received, Clear Lending unconditionally promises to pay each Holder the Redemption Amount of each Pool Share on its redemption under Article 6, and in any event on the Final Maturity Date, subject only to Sections 3.3, 3.4 and 5.6. Each Pool Share is a limited-recourse subordinated demand note of Clear Lending, represented by an ERC-4626 share of the Vault, denominated in dollars and paid one (1) USDC per dollar, or in dollars to Spendable if USDC cannot be delivered or is valued below par.

**3.2 Your Claim.** Your claim is the Redemption Amount of your Pool Shares. It equals the principal you paid for them, plus the Pool Interest credited to them, less the Pool Loss Charges allocated to them, plus any restoration under Section 5.4. It rises as Pool Interest accrues. It is written down only by a Pool Loss Charge for a Credit Loss actually realized, never by an estimate of future losses. A Pool Loss Allowance never reduces it; while one is posted, it only defers part of a redemption as a Loss Holdback under Section 5.6. Clear Lending's records show, for each Holder, the principal and the Pool Interest credited. Your claim can fall to zero.

**3.3 Limited Recourse.** Clear Lending's obligations on Pool Shares are payable only from the Pool Assets. Holders have no claim on the BondVault, the Redemption Reserve, the Loss Reserve, Residual Capital, Member collateral or any other asset outside the Pool Account. Once the Pool Assets have been applied as this Agreement and L-01 require, any remaining claim on the Pool Shares is extinguished. This Section does not limit Section 14.2(f).

**3.4 Ranking.** Pool Shares rank equally with one another. Under Section 17.4 of L-01, each Holder's claim is subordinated to payment in full of every BurnerBond, and Holdings' claim to the Pool Class. By holding a Pool Share you agree to that subordination, which is a subordination agreement under 11 U.S.C. § 510(a). On winding up, Pool Shares are paid under Section 17.3 of L-01, after general creditors and before Holdings.

**3.5 No Guarantee; No Vote.** No one guarantees the Pool Shares, and no Affiliate of Clear Lending, including Clear and Holdings, must pay them or contribute capital. Pool Shares are not deposits, are not part of your Equity Savings Account, and are not insured. A Pool Share carries no vote, membership interest or patronage right; its only consent rights are in Article 12.

**3.6 Article 8 Securities.** Each Pool Share expressly provides, and Clear Lending and each Holder agree, that it is a security governed by Article 8 of the Uniform Commercial Code as in effect in Wyoming (W.S. 34.1-8-101 and following) and every other jurisdiction, and a digital security under W.S. 34-29-102. It is an uncertificated security in registered form. Wyoming is the issuer's jurisdiction under W.S. 34.1-8-110(d).

**3.7 The Register.** The Pool Share holdings recorded in the Vault are Clear Lending's register of Pool Shares (the "**Register**") for all purposes, including Article 8. The registered owner is the Member or Successor whose Smart Account holds the Pool Share, as linked in Clear Lending's off-chain records under I-05.

**3.8 Final Maturity.** From the Final Maturity Date, Clear Lending makes no Deployment and applies all Returns and Vault Cash to redeem every Pool Share at its Redemption Amount as Pool Assets are realized. Any amount that is not paid on the Final Maturity Date is in payment default under Section 14.2.

## Article 4. Use of Proceeds, Deployment and Pool Interest

**4.1 Pool Tiers.** Pool Assets fund only principal of these Member credit products under the Credit Policy (the "**Pool Tiers**"): the income-backed line; Clear Boost; Partner Credit, including Funding Obligations paid into the PayoutPool under I-09; and Clear Cash; and Margin Advances under Section 4.4. Undeployed Pool Assets stay in the Vault as USDC.

**4.2 What Pool Assets Never Fund.** Pool Assets never fund savings-backed or asset-backed credit; credit to Clear, an Affiliate or an Insider outside standard Member terms, including under I-06 (any Interim Tranche Clear Lending holds is funded only from Residual Capital, within its fifteen percent (15%) non-consumer headroom under L-01 Section 2.9(b)); the special-purpose lender wholly owned by Holdings under I-06, which Clear Lending never funds or guarantees; credit to a non-Member; the BondVault, the Redemption Reserve, the Loss Reserve or the AssurancePool; Operating Expenses, fees or distributions; or any other investment.

**4.3 Deployment.** Clear Lending may make a Deployment only to its lending account at [●], under two-person control under Section 6.4 of L-01, while no Queue Request is open and no Suspension is in effect, and only if afterwards Utilization is no more than ninety percent (90%) and the Deployed Balance does not exceed the outstanding principal of the Pool Tiers plus Margin Advances. Clear Lending will return any excess over that last limit on the next Business Day.

**4.4 Margin Advances.** Clear Lending may deploy Pool Assets to fund the Savings-Backed Margin under Section 8.4 of L-01 (a "**Margin Advance**"), up to its target level less the part funded from other sources. A Margin Advance is held in Liquid Assets, never lent, bears no credit loss and earns Pool Interest. Once a Queue Request has been open thirty (30) days, Clear Lending will repay Margin Advances, except to the extent that would take the Savings-Backed Margin below its minimum.

**4.5 Returns.** While a Queue Request is open, Clear Lending will, each Business Day, return to the Vault the principal collected on the Pool Tiers, multiplied by the Deployed Balance (excluding Margin Advances) divided by the outstanding principal of the Pool Tiers, the fraction capped at one (1), subject only to Steps 1 and 2 of Section 9.2 of L-01. It may make a Return at any time.

**4.6 Pool Interest.** Pool Interest accrues each second on the Deployed Balance at the Funding Rate then in effect, on a three hundred sixty-five (365) day year. It is owed whether or not borrowers pay carry, and Credit Losses reduce it only through Article 5. Within five (5) Business Days after each month ends, Clear Lending will pay the month's Pool Interest into the Vault in USDC, at Step 3 of Section 9.2 of L-01, as liquidity this Agreement makes available to the Pool Class. Pool Interest unpaid when due is added to the Deployed Balance. Pool Interest is a cost of funds deducted in computing Spread, which Clear Lending keeps, and in which Holders have no share.

**4.7 Vault Discipline.** The Vault holds only USDC, never lent other than by Deployment, and never pledged, rehypothecated or invested in decentralized-finance protocols, derivatives or leverage. It has no sweep, rescue or administrative transfer function other than Deployment, redemption and settlement of Pool Loss Charges.

## Article 5. Losses, Share Value and Loss Holdback

**5.1 Order of Loss Absorption.** Every Credit Loss of Clear Lending, on any credit however funded, is absorbed in the order in Section 10.1 of L-01, each layer exhausted before the next bears any part:

(a) first, the Spread for the period in which the loss is recognized;

(b) second, the whole of the Loss Reserve;

(c) third, as to charged-off principal only, Draws from the Credit Backstop Compartment of Clear's AssurancePool under I-11, up to Available Coverage and never beyond it;

(d) fourth, Residual Capital;

(e) fifth, the Pool Class, by Pool Loss Charges applied first to Holdback Amounts under Section 5.6 and then pro rata among all Pool Shares; and

(f) last, BurnerBonds, which are never written down and bear a loss only as unpaid creditors under Section 10.2 of L-01.

Only the part of a loss on secured credit that the Member's collateral does not cover is a Credit Loss.

**5.2 The Credit Backstop.** The Credit Backstop Compartment belongs to Clear, not Clear Lending. It covers only charged-off principal, up to Available Coverage when a Draw is paid. It may be exhausted, Clear need not hold a balance or refill it, and coverage ends prospectively if I-11 ends. It is not insurance or a guarantee or promise of Clear or its Members, and you have no right under I-11.

**5.3 Pool Loss Charges.** A Credit Loss left after layers (a) to (d) is a Pool Loss Charge under Exhibit A. Because the Pool Class stands behind the whole credit book, it can bear losses on credit it did not fund.

**5.4 Recoveries.** Recoveries restore the layers in reverse order under Section 10.4 of L-01, the Pool Class first, by adding back to the Deployed Balance the Pool Loss Charges not yet restored.

**5.5 Share Value.** The "**Share Value**" is Pool Assets divided by the outstanding Pool Shares: in substance, principal plus Pool Interest accrued, less realized Pool Loss Charges, per Pool Share. The Vault reports it at all times and uses it as the price of every subscription and redemption, so the Redemption Amount of each Pool Share is its Share Value. The Pool Loss Allowance never enters the Share Value. If realized losses exhaust layers (a) to (d) of Section 5.1, the Share Value falls, possibly to zero.

**5.6 Loss Holdback.** While the Holdback Percentage is above zero, the following applies (the "**Loss Holdback**"):

(a) *Holdback.* Every redemption of Pool Shares, whether on demand, by a Queue fill, on enforcement under MB-05, by a Successor or from the Final Maturity Date, pays the Redemption Amount less the Holdback Percentage then posted. The part withheld (a "**Holdback Amount**") stays in the Vault, recorded to that Holder with its gross Redemption Amount. It is never deployed, earns no Pool Interest, and is not Vault Cash or Pool Assets.

(b) *Release.* Each time a lower Holdback Percentage is posted, every Holdback Amount above its gross Redemption Amount multiplied by the new Holdback Percentage is reduced to that level, and the excess is paid to the Holder's Smart Account. When the Holdback Percentage is zero, every Holdback Amount is paid in full. A higher posting never increases a Holdback Amount already recorded.

(c) *Maximum age.* A Holdback Amount not applied to a Pool Loss Charge posted within the Maximum Holdback Age after it was recorded is paid in full to the Holder's Smart Account. The "**Maximum Holdback Age**" is five hundred forty (540) days, a governed parameter of the Vault. Clear Lending may shorten it at any time, and a shorter Maximum Holdback Age applies to every Holdback Amount then open. Lengthening it needs the consent of the Majority Holders and applies only to Holdback Amounts recorded after it takes effect, never to one already open. A Pool Loss Charge posted before a Holdback Amount reaches the Maximum Holdback Age still applies to it under clause (d). Releases under this clause are processed in the order the Holdback Amounts were recorded, by anyone calling the Vault's release function.

(d) *Application.* When a Pool Loss Charge is posted, each Holdback Amount bears its ratable share first: its gross Redemption Amount multiplied by the Pool Loss Charge, divided by the sum of Pool Assets before the charge and the gross Redemption Amounts of all Holdback Amounts then open, but never more than the Holdback Amount. The whole Pool Loss Charge reduces the Deployed Balance, and the amounts so applied are added to Pool Assets as Vault Cash, so the remaining Pool Shares bear only the part of the Pool Loss Charge that Holdback Amounts do not. The Holder bears nothing beyond the Holdback Amount.

(e) *Nature.* A Holdback Amount is part of the Holder's Redemption Amount, deferred and payable only from that Holdback Amount. It is not due, and not in default, while this Section holds it. It is never available to Clear Lending, to other Holders or to any creditor except as clause (d) applies it. Holdback Amounts are released and applied under two-person control under Section 6.4 of L-01, and released amounts may be paid by anyone calling the Vault's release function.

This Section treats Holders who leave and Holders who stay alike: a Holder who redeems while losses are expected but not yet realized leaves the expected share behind, and receives it back if the losses do not occur.

## Article 6. Redemptions

**6.1 On Demand When Liquid.** You may redeem any of your Pool Shares whenever Vault Cash is available and no Queue Request is open, at the Redemption Amount when the redemption executes, less any Holdback Amount under Section 5.6, with the USDC paid to your Smart Account in the same transaction.

**6.2 Take Now, Queue the Rest.** If you ask for more than the Vault can pay at once, the App offers: "Take $X now, queue the rest." If you accept, the Vault pays $X and records a request to redeem the rest (a "**Queue Request**") in its redemption queue (the "**Queue**").

**6.3 How the Queue Works.**

(a) Queue Requests are filled first in, first out, by recorded time, and no one, including an Insider or Clear Lending as secured party, may move ahead.

(b) Each Queue Request is filled at the Share Value when filled, not when made, less any Holdback Amount under Section 5.6, and until then its Pool Shares keep earning and bearing losses.

(c) All Vault Cash, including from subscriptions, Returns and Pool Interest, goes first to the Queue, and no immediate redemption is possible while it is open.

(d) Anyone may call the function that fills Queue Requests, in order, in whole or in part, up to the Vault Cash available. You may cancel all or part of an unfilled Queue Request at any time.

**6.4 Extended Queue.** Once a Queue Request has been open thirty (30) days, Clear Lending makes no distribution to Holdings until the Queue is clear. At ninety (90) days, the Lending Board will meet within five (5) Business Days and send Holders a written liquidity plan. No Holder may require Clear Lending to sell loans, borrow or use assets outside the Pool Account to fill the Queue. A Reserve Shortfall does not pause redemptions or the Queue, as Section 9.3 of L-01 permits.

**6.5 Pledges and Proceeds.** A pledged Pool Share may be redeemed only as far as MB-05 permits its release, and an enforcement redemption joins the Queue like any other. Proceeds go only to the Smart Account that held the Pool Shares, except enforcement proceeds under MB-05 and payments to a Successor.

**6.6 No Payments From Pool Shares.** Pool Shares are not a means of payment. No card transaction, Send under MB-09, bill payment, ACH debit or automatic clearing under MB-04 is ever paid from a Pool Share or from the Vault. To use the value of a Pool Share you must first redeem it under this Article, and the proceeds are paid into your Ready to allocate (or, under Section 3.1, in dollars to Spendable). Clear Lending does not describe Pool Shares, in the App or elsewhere, as a deposit, an account, savings, an interest-bearing account or a balance available on demand, and does not state any yield for them as an annual percentage yield.

## Article 7. Suspension

**7.1 Suspension Events.** Clear Lending may suspend subscriptions, redemptions and Queue processing (a "**Suspension**") only while one of these continues:

(a) a loss event under investigation: Clear Lending reasonably believes that a fraud, security incident, or servicing, data or valuation error may change the Share Value or the Holdback Percentage by more than one percent (1%), and is investigating;

(b) a court, regulator or law-enforcement authority with jurisdiction over Clear Lending, Clear Financial Co. or the Vault orders or directs it;

(c) USDC is valued below par under the definition of Liquid Assets, or the Vault's network is halted or not finalizing transactions; or

(d) a Valuation Event has occurred and the updated Pool Loss Allowance has not yet been posted.

**7.2 Never a Suspension Event.** Lack of liquidity (which the Queue addresses), a Reserve Shortfall, a Credit Policy status change, market conditions, credit or collection purposes, and any need of Clear Lending, Holdings or Clear are never Suspension Events.

**7.3 Two-Person Control and Time Limits.** A Suspension is set by one authorized individual and approved by a different one, at least one of whom is Clear Lending's chief compliance officer or treasurer, with a reason code recorded on-chain. It expires after seventy-two (72) hours unless renewed by a different signer, with the reason logged. It may not continue beyond ten (10) Business Days without a Lending Board resolution with the Independent Manager's vote, or beyond thirty (30) days in total for the same event, except under Section 7.1(b) as long as the order requires. A Suspension under Section 7.1(d) lasts no more than two (2) Business Days. A new Suspension on the same facts within thirty (30) days needs the same resolution.

**7.4 During and After.** No Deployment is made, the Queue keeps its order, you may still cancel a Queue Request, Pool Interest keeps accruing, and Returns accumulate in the Vault. Before a Suspension ends, Clear Lending posts any required Pool Loss Allowance or Pool Loss Charge, and the Queue is then processed first. Clear Lending tells Holders in the App and by email within twenty-four (24) hours after a Suspension begins, with its reason and expected length, and on each renewal and its end.

**7.5 Holds Required by Law.** If the law, including regulations of the Office of Foreign Assets Control, requires Clear Lending to block a Pool Share or payment, it will do so only for that Holder and only for as long as the law requires.

## Article 8. Transfers and Pledges

**8.1 No Transfers.** You may not transfer a Pool Share or any interest in it, except to Clear Lending by redemption, by pledge to Clear Lending under MB-05, or by operation of law under Section 8.3. Any other purported transfer is void. There is no secondary market, and Clear Lending will not create or recognize one. The Vault rejects every other transfer, except between two Smart Accounts registered to the same Holder.

**8.2 Pledge Under MB-05.** You may pledge Pool Shares to Clear Lending as collateral for asset-backed credit under MB-05, and no one else. Clear Lending, as issuer, agrees with you that it will comply with instructions originated by Clear Lending, as secured party under MB-05, about your pledged Pool Shares without your further consent, under Uniform Commercial Code Section 8-106(c)(2), and you consent to that agreement. The Register records each pledge, and a pledged Pool Share stays in your Smart Account under the Pledge Module. Its collateral value is the percentage of Pool Share Value in Section 5.1(c) of MB-05, initially seventy percent (70%). Pool Share Value is what the Vault would pay to redeem the Pool Share, which is its Redemption Amount less any Loss Holdback then applying. A pledged Pool Share is enforced only by redemption through the Vault, in the Queue if one is open. MB-05 controls if it conflicts with this Agreement on a pledged Pool Share.

**8.3 Transfer by Operation of Law.** On a Holder's death, incapacity, divorce or bankruptcy, Pool Shares pass to the person entitled under the law (the "**Successor**"). Once the Successor gives proof of entitlement under W.S. 34.1-8-402, completes identity verification and gives a Form W-9, Clear Lending will register the Successor as Holder. If the Holder's Smart Account cannot be accessed, Clear Lending will redeem the Pool Shares and pay the Successor in dollars, after receiving the indemnity W.S. 34.1-8-405 permits.

**8.4 Legend.** The Register and the Vault's metadata carry this legend: "These Pool Shares have not been registered under the Securities Act of 1933 or any state securities law. They may not be transferred except to Clear Lending Co. or by operation of law, as IV-02 permits."

**8.5 Clear Lending's Powers.** Clear Lending holds no key, role or session key that can move, burn or freeze a Pool Share in your Smart Account. Its only powers over your Pool Shares are redemptions you sign, enforcement under MB-05, a Suspension, registration of a Successor, holds under Section 7.5, redemption from the Final Maturity Date, and the posting, release and application of Holdback Amounts under Section 5.6.

## Article 9. Fees, Costs and Reporting

**9.1 No Fees.** No subscription, sales, management, performance, redemption, queue, inactivity or other fee is charged to Holders or to the Pool Assets.

## Schedule 2. Interest Rate Model

**1. Formula.** With Utilization U (zero if the Vault is empty), the Funding Rate R is an annual simple rate:

- if U ≤ K: R = R₀ + S₁ × (U ÷ K)
- if U > K: R = R₀ + S₁ + S₂ × ((U − K) ÷ (1 − K))

where R₀ is the base rate, S₁ the slope to the kink, K the kink and S₂ the slope above it. The Vault recomputes R whenever the Deployed Balance or Vault Cash changes.

**2. Parameters and Bounds.**

| Parameter | Initial value | Bounds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base rate R₀ | 2.00% | 0.00% to 5.00% |
| Slope to kink S₁ | 3.00% | 0.00% to 6.00% |
| Kink K | 80% | 60% to 90% |
| Slope above kink S₂ | 5.00% | 0.00% to 10.00% |
| Maximum R₀ + S₁ + S₂ | 10.00% | Never above 10.00% |

**3. Examples at the Initial Parameters.**

| Utilization | Funding Rate | Holder yield before losses (about R × U) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0% | 2.00% | 0.00% |
| 50% | 3.88% | 1.94% |
| 80% | 5.00% | 4.00% |
| 90% | 7.50% | 6.75% |
| 100% | 10.00% | 10.00% |

**4. Changes.** Only the Lending Board, by resolution with the Independent Manager's vote, sets the parameters within the bounds. Each change runs through the Vault's timelock under two-person control and is published on the Earn page. A change that does not lower the Funding Rate at any Utilization takes effect after at least forty-eight (48) hours. A change that lowers it at any Utilization takes effect only after at least thirty (30) days' notice to Holders, during which they may redeem, and only one such change may be made in a calendar quarter. A change to the bounds is an amendment under Section 12.2.

## Exhibit A. Loss Holdback and Impairment Policy

**A1. Standard and Components.** This Policy treats Holders who subscribe, stay and leave alike, using conservative estimates and the same method every time. The Share Value is not a market value. Vault Cash is valued at par unless the USDC exception in the definition of Liquid Assets applies. The Deployed Balance is carried at its recorded amount. The Pool Loss Allowance never changes the Share Value. It sets only the Holdback Percentage under Section 5.6.

**A2. Pool Loss Charges.** At each month end, Clear Lending applies the month's Credit Losses through Section 5.1(a) to (d). A Draw Request recorded under I-11 within Available Coverage counts as absorbed until finally refused. Any Credit Loss that remains is a "**Pool Loss Charge**". It is applied first to open Holdback Amounts under Section 5.6(d), which adds the amounts applied to Vault Cash. The whole Pool Loss Charge reduces the Deployed Balance and, to the extent it exceeds the Deployed Balance and accrued Pool Interest, is settled by transfer from the Vault to Clear Lending under two-person control. Each Pool Loss Charge is posted, with its computation and the amounts applied from Holdback Amounts, within five (5) Business Days after month end.

**A3. Pool Loss Allowance.** The "**Pool Loss Allowance**" is the Loss Estimate less the Senior Loss Capacity, but not less than zero or more than Pool Assets, where:

(a) the "**Loss Estimate**" is the sum of (i) Credit Losses recognized and not yet absorbed or charged under Section A2; (ii) the unpaid principal and carry, net of collateral at its advance rate, of every account ninety (90) or more days past due and not charged off; and (iii) twelve (12) months of Expected Credit Loss under Section 6.1 of the Credit Policy on the rest of Clear Lending's credit book, treating every I-06 loan as unsecured; and

(b) the "**Senior Loss Capacity**" is the sum of (i) the Liquid Assets in the Loss Reserve; (ii) Available Coverage, up to the principal in the Loss Estimate; (iii) Residual Capital; and (iv) if positive, fifty percent (50%) of Spread less Operating Expenses for the last three (3) months, multiplied by four (4).

The Redemption Reserve, the BondVault, the Savings-Backed Margin and the Member Assurance Compartment never count as Senior Loss Capacity. The Holdback Percentage is the posted Pool Loss Allowance divided by Pool Assets at the same time.

**A4. Timing.** Clear Lending posts the Pool Loss Allowance and the Holdback Percentage as at each month end within five (5) Business Days, and an update within two (2) Business Days after any "**Valuation Event**": (a) a change to Equity or Stop status under the Credit Policy; (b) Liquid Assets in the Loss Reserve below fifty percent (50%) of the Timing Buffer; (c) notice ending I-11, or a fall of twenty-five percent (25%) or more in Available Coverage within a month; (d) a fraud, security, servicing or data event estimated to affect Pool Assets by one percent (1%) or more; (e) a change in the valuation of USDC; or (f) any other fact the treasurer believes changes the Holdback Percentage by more than one-half of one percent (0.5%).

**A5. Controls.** The chief credit officer certifies the credit inputs. The treasurer computes each posting, including each release and application of Holdback Amounts and each release at the Maximum Holdback Age under Section 5.6(c), and reconciles it to the monthly certificate under Section 3.9 of I-11. Each posting is made by one authorized individual and approved by another, with an evidence hash, and the Independent Manager reviews them quarterly.

**A6. Rounding.** The Vault rounds Pool Shares issued and USDC paid down, and rounds each Holdback Amount up and each release down, so rounding never favors a Holder who enters or leaves. It uses a virtual share offset, so USDC sent to the Vault other than through a subscription (a donation) can only raise the Share Value, ratably for all Holders, and can never cause a subscriber to receive fewer Pool Shares than the subscription buys at the Share Value then in effect, apart from rounding down.

**A7. Independent Review.** Each fiscal year, [●] (the "**Independent Accountant**"), which does not audit Clear Lending, performs agreed-upon procedures on Pool Loss Charges, the Pool Loss Allowance, the Holdback Percentage, Holdback Amounts, Pool Interest and the Funding Rate, and reports to the Lending Board and Holders.

**A8. Errors.** If an error changed the Share Value or a Holdback Amount by one-quarter of one percent (0.25%) or more, Clear Lending will correct it, pay any Holder who received too little, and pay into the Vault any amount overpaid to others, from its own funds. It does not recover overpayments from Holders.

**A9. Changes.** The Lending Board may change this Exhibit only by resolution with the Independent Manager's vote. A more conservative change takes effect when published. Any other change is an amendment under Section 12.2 or 12.3.

**A10. Worked Example.** Pool Assets are $100,000: Vault Cash $20,000 and Deployed Balance $80,000, so Utilization is 80% and the Funding Rate 5.00%. Interest credited since the Initial Closing has brought the Share Value to $1.04. The Loss Estimate is $24,000. The Loss Reserve holds $14,000, Available Coverage is $5,000, Residual Capital is $2,000 and the Spread credit is $1,500, so the Senior Loss Capacity is $22,500, the Pool Loss Allowance is $1,500 and the Holdback Percentage is 1.5%. The Share Value stays $1.04. A Holder redeems 10,000 Pool Shares, with a Redemption Amount of $10,400. The Holder receives $10,244, and $156 is held back in the Vault for that Holder. Next month the Holdback Percentage is posted at 0.5%, so the Holdback Amount falls to $52 and $104 is paid to the Holder. The month after, a $900 Pool Loss Charge is posted when Pool Assets are $89,600 and the only open holdback is this one ($10,400 gross). Its ratable share is $10,400 × $900 ÷ ($89,600 + $10,400) = $93.60, capped at $52, so the whole $52 is applied, and the remaining Pool Shares bear $848. If no loss had been realized and the Holdback Percentage had returned to zero, the Holder would have received the $52.

## Exhibit B. Earn Insider Trading Policy

**B1. Who and What.** This Policy binds every Insider. It covers every Insider Transaction: a subscription for, redemption of, or Queue Request or cancellation for Pool Shares; a purchase of a BurnerBond under IV-01; a Roll Confirmation under Section 5.1 of IV-01; and an offer of a BurnerBond for buyback under Section 4.8 of IV-01. Payment of a BurnerBond at maturity, a redemption or payment made under MB-05 enforcement, and a transfer by operation of law are not Insider Transactions.

**B2. Never While Aware.** No Insider may make an Insider Transaction, or advise anyone else to make one, while aware of material information about Clear Lending, the Pool or the BurnerBonds that has not been disclosed to Holders, including a Suspension Event, Valuation Event, Pool Loss Charge or Reserve Shortfall not yet disclosed, a proposed Pool Loss Allowance posting, or a regulatory or licensing matter.

**B3. Trading Window.** An Insider may make an Insider Transaction only during a trading window that opens when the monthly statement under Section 9.4 is delivered to Holders and closes at the end of the tenth (10th) Business Day after that, and only after the chief compliance officer of Clear Lending, or for the chief compliance officer the chief executive officer, has pre-cleared it in writing on the Business Day it is made. The window does not open, or closes at once, while Clear Lending is in a blackout the chief compliance officer declares because an undisclosed event of the kind in Section B2 exists; a blackout is kept confidential and ends when the event is disclosed. A BurnerBond whose Roll Deadline falls outside a window is paid at maturity unless the Insider has given a pre-cleared Roll Confirmation inside an earlier window.

**B4. Hardship.** The chief compliance officer may pre-clear a redemption outside a window for documented hardship, death or disability, if the Insider certifies in writing that it is not aware of any information described in Section B2.

**B5. Records and Reporting.** The chief compliance officer keeps every pre-clearance request, decision and certification for five (5) years and reports Insider Transactions in aggregate to the Lending Board each quarter. A breach is reported to the Lending Board and the Independent Manager, and the Insider pays Clear Lending any profit made or loss avoided by it.

**B6. Relationship to Other Rules.** This Policy adds to Section 10.7 of this Agreement, Section 2.4A of IV-01 and C-04, and does not limit the federal securities laws.
