# Deployment and upgrade runbook

This is the order in which the audit's contract changes reach Base Sepolia and then Base mainnet: what is upgraded in place, what is deployed new or replaced, why, which script does it, and what has to be wired, signed and waited for. It covers every phase merged into `audit/phase-2f-integration` (Phases 1, 2, 2b to 2g, 3, 4, 4b, 4c and 2f).

Nothing in this file has been run against a real network. No contract has been deployed, no key used, and no network touched; section 2 has been rehearsed on a local fork of Base Sepolia ([SEPOLIA-REHEARSAL.md](./SEPOLIA-REHEARSAL.md)). None of the audit's contract changes is on any chain yet: Base Sepolia runs the pre-audit contracts (`deployments/base-sepolia/`, `.openzeppelin/base-sepolia.json`), and Base mainnet runs `ClearUSD`, `ESADepositVault`, `ClaimEscrow` and `Create2Deployer` only (`deployments/base/`, `.openzeppelin/base.json`).

Where something is not in the repository (an address, a Safe, who holds a key, a figure), this file says **owner to confirm**.

**Decision 47 (owner, Nov 15, 2026): the Base Sepolia credit stack is deployed fresh, not upgraded in place** ([DEC](./specs/phase-2f-plan-decisions.md) 47). Old lines and plans have zero accrual snapshots and would be charged decades of carry on first touch (P1 §5 item 8; P4 §6 and BLIND-REVIEW I-1), and a fresh deploy makes Base Sepolia a rehearsal of mainnet. The plan below follows it. ESADepositVault, ClaimEscrow, ClearUSDUpgradeable, LendingPool (the Yield Pool) and BondVault are still upgraded in place, and re-pointed at the fresh stack where their setters allow.

**Decision 48 (owner, Nov 15, 2026): the retired contracts are disconnected, not paused or timelocked** ([DEC](./specs/phase-2f-plan-decisions.md) 48). The BondVault's `payoutPool` backstop is cleared by a co-signed `setBackstops(treasury, 0)` once the old PayoutPool's loan is repaid (deploy 27 prints it); every role a retired `*PreAudit`, `*Pre2f` or `*Legacy` contract holds on a kept contract is revoked (deploys 22, 23, 25 and 27); [`scripts/check_retired_credit.ts`](../../scripts/check_retired_credit.ts) checks all of it, read-only, and gates the app PRs; the `*Pre2f` and `*Legacy` collections and deposit contracts go to the Earn timelock with the new collection.

**Decision 50 (owner, Nov 15, 2026): on Base Sepolia the retired contracts' leftovers are test money and do not block** ([DEC](./specs/phase-2f-plan-decisions.md) 50). The old PayoutPool's open loans, its legs while a loan is open, the BondVault's `payoutPool` backstop while `lentToPayoutPool` is not zero, and balances left in retired contracts stand as they are: nothing is swept or repaid. `check_retired_credit.ts` reports each as a WARN ("test money, decision 50") on any chain but Base mainnet, where each would be a FAIL; roles, sources and refusals stay FAIL everywhere. Deploy 27 skips the backstop change on a testnet while a loan is open. The new `BurnerBondFactory` and every `*Pre2f` and `*Legacy` factory also move to the Earn timelock (step I4). Mainnet has no retired credit stack, so nothing there changes.

**What "storage confirmed" means here.** OpenZeppelin's `getStorageUpgradeReport` found the current layout compatible with the implementation(s) recorded for that contract in the network's `.openzeppelin/*.json`. The manifests do not say which recorded implementation each proxy runs today, so every check is against all of them. The scripts are [`scripts/storage_report.ts`](../../scripts/storage_report.ts) (any contract, by name; `STORAGE_MANIFEST=base` or `base-sepolia`), [`scripts/storage_report_lending_pool.ts`](../../scripts/storage_report_lending_pool.ts) and [`scripts/storage_report_bond_vault.ts`](../../scripts/storage_report_bond_vault.ts). All three are read-only and need no key or network.

**Rechecked for this runbook** on 2026-11-15 at `fbf55292`, with a local compile and no network: every result in the tables below was reproduced. For five contracts no earlier report had run the check against the Sepolia manifest (AccessManager, StableCredit for ClearCredit, LimitCalculator, OffersIssuer and DeedNFT); those results are marked "(this runbook)". ClearUSDUpgradeable was also rechecked directly, because `storage_report.ts` cannot match a layout that lives entirely in an ERC-7201 namespace.

### 4.3 Payout funding keeper (decision 49) and automatic top-ups (decision 51)

Do this after PayoutPool, the Yield Pool and BondVault are deployed and their addresses are in the API's config: `PAYOUT_POOL_<chainId>`, `LENDING_POOL_<chainId>` and `BOND_VAULT_<chainId>`, or the defaults in `apps/api/src/config/contracts.ts` (see §4.2). Until then the keeper watches the old pool.

1. Set `OPS_ALERT_EMAIL` on the API to the ops addresses, comma-separated. `DATABASE_URL` must be set too, because the `ops_alerts` table makes sure each alert goes out once. Without either, alerts are only logged.
2. Set `PAYOUT_KEEPER_ENABLED=1` and restart. The log should show `[payout-keeper] started (hourly, read-only)`, followed by a GREEN, AMBER or RED line. If a bank holiday falls inside the window, list it in `PAYOUT_KEEPER_HOLIDAYS` (YYYY-MM-DD, Pacific).
3. To check by hand at any time, run `npx hardhat run scripts/check_payout_funding.ts --network <network>`. It is read-only and exits 2 on RED.

**The standing-balance rule.** Clear Lending keeps PayoutPool's payable balance (what it holds, less refunds owed to members) at or above the claims due within the next 30 days.
- **AMBER** means the balance is below that. Top up the amount shown, no later than the date shown; that is when the first unfunded claim reaches its addendum 12.10 funding deadline.
- **RED** means a claim is unfunded within 2 business days of its payout deadline, or is already past it. Fund the amount shown now. If the pool already holds the money, call `payNext()`.

Funding comes from a recorded Yield Pool Deployment, from BondVault excess withdrawn through the Collateral Agent, or from the treasury. The keeper reports how much each option has available. It moves nothing, holds no key and sends no transactions.

**Automatic top-ups (decision 51).** Routine top-ups can run on their own, within a limit. A separate job (`apps/api/src/jobs/payoutTopUp.ts`, off unless `PAYOUT_TOPUP_ENABLED=1`) checks every 15 minutes. It moves money only when the claims due within 14 days cannot all be paid, and then refills to the claims due within 30 days plus a margin. The money comes out of CL Operating through Safe's AllowanceModule, whose spending limit ($5,000 a week on Base Sepolia) caps everything the job can move. The keeper above is unchanged and keeps alerting. Full detail: [`docs/operators/payout-topups.md`](../operators/payout-topups.md).

4. **Day 0, before I2:** the deployer sends `PT-funder` from the signing plan, which grants the fresh PayoutPool's `FUNDER_ROLE` to the top-up wallet `0xC0C4EC7875D1838C0AE610accC16AAdCDb475068` (CDP `payout-topup-base-sepolia`). After I2 this is a 7-day credit timelock proposal instead.
5. CL Operating's owner, the Clear Lending Safe, signs one Transaction Builder batch on CL Operating: `safe-batches/signing-PT-cl-operating-setup.json`, the plan's `PT-safe-module`, `PT-safe-delegate` and `PT-safe-allowance`. It enables the module, names the wallet a delegate, and sets its allowance.
6. Check the setup read-only with `scripts/check_rollout_inputs.ts`. Point `PAYOUT_POOL_84532` at the fresh PayoutPool (§4.2), then set these on the API and restart: `PAYOUT_TOPUP_WALLET`, `PAYOUT_TOPUP_CDP_ACCOUNT_NAME=payout-topup-base-sepolia`, then `PAYOUT_TOPUP_ENABLED=1`. The log shows `[payout-topup] started`.
7. Day to day: alerts say when CL Operating needs money, or when its owners must sign a remainder the limit could not cover (the batch is in the alert and on the Command Center). Any revert stops the job until a person clears it with `bun src/scripts/clearPayoutTopUpHalt.ts`.
